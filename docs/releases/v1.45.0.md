Introduction videos and captions in Video, the tools of a photo editor in Image, and passwords that no longer hold up the other windows.

## Worksheets and Documents

- **Checking or setting a password no longer holds up the other
  windows.** Protect and Unprotect Sheet and Workbook, Allow Edit Ranges and
  Unlock Range, Restrict Editing's Start Enforcing Protection and Stop
  Protection, Encrypt with Password, and opening a file encrypted with a
  password all hash the password 100,000 times or more, as Office does.
  That work now runs on threads of its own, and every other open document
  goes on taking your typing while it runs. The window that asked shows it
  is working: its OK is held with a small spinner and says "Checking…",
  and a wrong password is still refused in Excel's or Word's own words.
  Reopening a recovered copy of an encrypted document is unlocked the same
  way. The files written are the same as before, byte for byte, and the
  hashing is a third faster.

## Image

- **The tools of a photo editor**: fourteen tools stand in a column down
  the left of the picture, with the two colours at its foot, and the chosen
  tool's options sit in one bar above it. In Urdu and Arabic the column
  stands on the right.
- **Selections**: a rectangle, an ellipse, a lasso, the magic wand or by
  colour, added to, taken from or intersected, with Feather, Grow and
  Shrink under Modify. With a selection, the Adjust and Filters tabs change
  only what is selected, on a layer of its own.
- **Painting**: the brush paints on a paint layer of its own, the eraser
  rubs out, and the fill and the gradient stay inside the selection. The
  clone stamp copies from where you Alt+click, and the healing brush covers
  a blemish with clean skin matched to the colour round it.
- **Masks**: a layer's mask reveals all, hides all or is made from the
  selection, painted in black and white, feathered, inverted, turned off,
  applied or deleted, and kept in the .rimg project.
- **Free Transform** drags a picture's corners to scale, skew, distort or
  add perspective, and **Perspective** takes four pins on something
  photographed at an angle and straightens it, cropped or not.
- Fixed: the Image window's empty state nested a block inside a paragraph,
  which React warned about.

## Video

- **Introduction videos**: Introduction video in the launcher, or New
  Introduction Video in Video, opens a project of scenes: a title card, you
  on camera, your screen with the camera in a corner, and an end card, with
  side by side as a fifth layout. Each scene is a card in the Scenes panel
  and on the Introduction tab. Scenes move earlier or later, are added and
  are taken out, and a recorded scene's layout can be changed afterwards,
  because the screen and the camera stay separate.
- **A teleprompter**: write a scene's script and it scrolls above the
  camera while you record, slow, normal or fast. Record a scene, take it
  again, and put any earlier take back; every take is kept.
- **A brand kit** keeps your name and role, a logo, three colours and a
  font for every introduction you make. It draws the cards in your colours
  and puts a lower third with your name over the first recorded scene, out
  of the camera's way, and right to left for an Urdu or Arabic name.
- **The pointer**: while Video records, it keeps where the pointer was and
  where you clicked in the suite's own windows. On the timeline that is a
  layer of its own that draws a highlight and a ring at each click, either
  of which can be turned off afterwards. Zoom on Clicks zooms the screen in
  where you clicked and follows the clicks, and each zoom can be made
  closer or taken away in the clip's properties.
- **Captions**: Import Captions reads a SubRip (.srt) or WebVTT (.vtt) file
  onto a track of its own, each line a clip moved, trimmed and edited like
  a title, drawn right to left in Urdu and Arabic, and Export Captions
  writes it back.
- **Export for where it is going**: YouTube at 1080p 16:9, LinkedIn and X
  at 16:9 or square, Reels, Shorts and TikTok at 9:16, and a small MP4 for
  email, each with its frame size, rate and likely file size. An upright
  export lays every scene out for that shape and leaves the project as it
  was.
