Rutba Office 2.27.0 adds group conversations and small meetings to Talk, an icon beside the clock with Windows notifications, guests from Talk and cameras on the network in Live Broadcast and the Recorder, more kinds of layer in Video, and printing from Mail, Calendar, Contacts, Image and Pictures.

## Talk

- **What waits is counted.** Each conversation shows how many messages
  have not been read on this computer, and Talk's tile on the start
  screen shows the total.
- **Missed calls.** A call that reached nobody is listed as missed,
  whether it rang here or was made while this computer was off. Calls
  from the same person fold into one entry with a count.
- **Sending, sent, arrived.** A message of yours says which of the three
  it is. One that was on its way when the connection dropped is sent
  again. One that could not be sent says why, with Try again and Remove.
- **Group conversations.** Three or more people in one conversation.
  Each person is put there by one of their own contacts.
- **One call at a time.** A second call while one is ringing or going on
  is turned away as busy and kept as a missed call, and its line shows in
  the conversation at once.
- **Talk connects when Office starts**, for someone signed in to Talk,
  without its window being opened. A setting turns that off.
- **Rings.** The Talk window rings for a call coming in, plays a soft
  tone while a call rings at the other side, and a short tone when you
  are asked into a meeting. A second setting turns the sounds off.
- **A call rings with no Talk window open.** Office then rings from its
  icon beside the clock until the call is answered, declined or over.
  Once a Talk window comes forward, the window rings and the other stops.
- **Settings and the people you blocked.** Talk has a Settings page with
  the two settings and the people blocked, each by name with Unblock.
- **Report someone to Rutba.** Their last messages go with the report
  only when you tick so. Nothing is ticked beforehand.
- **No place to type a server.** Up to 2.26.1 Talk's sign-in offered
  "Connect to a test server" with boxes for an address and a token.
  Office is given its servers by Rutba and those boxes are gone.
- **Sign-in says more.** With no connection it says what to check. The
  wait for the browser can be cancelled, or the browser opened again.

### Meetings

- **A meeting of up to four people**, with sound and picture going
  between the computers themselves. A tile that could not connect says
  who and why, with Try again.
- **Meet now** from a person or a group conversation.
- **A meeting on a Calendar event.** Join the meeting is on the event's
  card and on Mail's invitation card. Calendar connects Talk before it
  sets a meeting up, and says why when it cannot.
- **Before you join** you choose the microphone and camera and see your
  own picture. A meeting that is full, locked or over refuses before any
  microphone opens. A microphone or camera that cannot be used is said in
  words.
- **Streamed or recorded is said at the door.** Whether a meeting is
  streamed live is chosen when it is set up and cannot be changed later.
  Everyone asked in reads it, and who does it, before they join and all
  through the meeting.
- **Joining by link uses the meeting's code alone.** Office asks the
  server it is signed in to and never an address a link names.
- **Who can join** is the organiser's Talk contacts, and Calendar, the
  copied link, the join screen and the invitation each say so.
- **The organiser's red button asks**: leave, or end for everyone.

### A Talk window is not captured

- A Talk window that holds a call or a meeting is left out of the list of
  windows for a screenshot, a recording or a broadcast, and is refused
  when asked for by another window.
- A call, or a meeting not set up to be streamed or recorded, is also
  kept out of a capture of the whole screen: the Talk window shows as a
  blank rectangle there for as long as the call lasts.

## An icon beside the clock, and notifications

- **Rutba Office has an icon beside the clock.** Its menu lists what
  waits: missed calls, unread messages, new mail and today's events,
  each opening the place it is about. Below them are Show Rutba Office,
  Pause notifications for an hour, the notification settings and Quit.
- **Windows notifications** for a call, a missed call, a message, new
  mail, an event's reminder and a meeting about to start. Each kind is
  on or off in File, Notifications, with sound, quiet hours and whether
  the words are shown.
- **A notification opens the place it is about.** Answer on a call's
  notification opens Talk with the call still ringing. It answers
  nothing by itself, so no microphone opens until you press Answer in
  the window.
- **Office stays by the clock while something can arrive**: Talk signed
  in, a mail account that is fetched, a reminder or a meeting to come.
  With nothing to watch for, closing the last window quits as before.
- **Start with Windows** is offered on the installed copy.
- **Updates.** An update that has come down is installed when the last
  window closes, or at once when no window is open, and Office comes back
  to the clock without opening a window.
- Uninstalling removes this copy's Start with Windows entries.

## Live Broadcast and the Recorder

### Guests from Talk

- **Up to three Talk contacts can be asked in as guests.** Each takes a
  seat, and a seat is a source like a camera: a scene places it and the
  mix carries its sound.
- **A guest waits off air** until their seat is in the frame of a
  broadcast or a recording that is running. Their sound goes out only
  then. They see the programme all the while.
- **Everyone asked in is told first.** The window holds a small Talk
  meeting set up to be streamed live or to be recorded, and each guest
  reads that before joining.
- **Keep a copy and guests.** With Keep a copy on before anyone is asked,
  Live Broadcast's meeting is set up as recorded too and guests read both
  notices. While a meeting set up to be streamed only is in the window,
  Keep a copy is greyed and says why.
- **Computer sound is held out of the mix while a guest is heard on this
  computer**, and its row says why. Otherwise the guest's voice would go
  out twice, or go out while they wait off air.
- A meeting set up in Talk to be streamed opens in Live Broadcast, and
  goes back to Talk.

### Cameras on the network

- **Add, Camera by address** takes an RTSP address and a sign-in.
- **Add, Cameras on the network** asks the local network for cameras
  that speak ONVIF, and asks the one you choose for its stream.
- Office reads the camera itself (H.264 over RTSP, with Digest or Basic
  sign-in). No other program is installed or run for it.
- A camera's password is kept in Windows' own keystore and is never
  handed to a window.

### A clean start, and setups

- The Recorder and Live Broadcast start in a new session that is written
  nowhere until you save it as a setup under a name. Reset returns to a
  new session. Saved setups stay as they are.
- **A session you arranged is asked about before it is left.** Closing
  the window, or opening a setup over it, asks "Keep this session?" with
  Save as a setup, Don't save and Cancel. A session nobody touched is
  not asked about.
- With setups saved, a line at the top of a new session says they are
  not open, with a menu to open one.

## Video

- **More kinds of layer.** Paragraphs and lists, overlays, colour looks,
  a progress bar, a fade at the two ends and an outro card. Each is a
  clip on the timeline.
- **An audio library** keeps sound and video files for every project,
  from this computer or from an https address. A file fetched from the
  web is marked as from the internet. A fetch shows in the list with
  Cancel, and a sound that cannot be had says which of three things went
  wrong: the file is gone, it cannot be played, or it could not be
  fetched.
- **A QR code is added with no address.** A box on the frame says "Type a
  web address" until you type one, so no made-up address ends up in a
  film.
- An end card can be picked on the preview once it has faded in.
- **Movement over time.** Points set at the playhead show as diamonds on
  the clip, and the layer travels between them.
- More transitions, and a way in and a way out for every kind of layer.

## Printing

- **One print dialog.** Print sends the job straight to the printer
  chosen in Office's dialog, on the paper chosen there. In 2.26.1 the
  system's dialog opened after ours and asked for the printer and the
  paper a second time. The system's dialog is still there, behind the
  "System dialog…" button, for what only a printer's driver knows, such
  as two sides or a tray.
- The dialog names what is about to print and shows its first page small.
- The list of printers opens on the system's default printer, and says
  beside a printer that it is offline, paused or out of paper.
- **Pages.** Type page numbers and ranges, as in 1,2,5,6 or 1-5. Only
  those sheets go to the printer or into the PDF.
- **Automatic orientation.** A workbook too wide for upright paper, a
  picture wider than it is tall and a calendar's month or week go
  sideways unless you choose a way.
- A print the printer does not take leaves the dialog open and says so,
  so another printer or Save as PDF can be tried.
- With no printer installed the dialog says so, and Save as PDF works.
- Not yet: the dialog opens on A4 each time, and the PDF app and Data
  Files still print through the system's dialog.
- **Mail, Calendar, Contacts, Image and Pictures print.** The open
  message, the calendar's view, a contact's card or the shown list, the
  picture in Image and the shown pictures each print through the same
  dialog, to a printer or to a PDF.
- A printed message draws the pictures it carries and loads nothing
  else: no picture from the web and no file on this computer.
- A page from the Locker prints from memory and is not written as a PDF
  outside the Locker.
- **PDF: Print sends every page.** In 2.26.1 a PDF of several pages sent
  one sheet to the printer. A Data Files report and print template had
  the same fault and are put right with it.
- **PDF: Print says what it is doing.** On a long file the window says
  which page it is preparing, with Cancel beside it, and says when the
  job has been sent.

## Read Aloud

- The bar is a strip over the page or the slide. In 2.26.1 it stood as a
  column beside the page in Documents and Presentations.
- Documents shows the sentence being read.
- **A key pressed while Documents reads no longer deletes the
  sentence.** Space pauses and reads on, Escape stops, and any other
  edit is told "Stop reading to change the words."
- **When no voice reads the language** the bar says so, for example "No
  voice for Urdu is on this computer.", beside a button that opens
  Voices and languages.

## Installing

- An install that put nothing on the computer now ends with an error
  that says what is missing. After 2.26.1 an update run without
  administrator rights, over what was left of a copy for all users,
  could end as if it had worked with no Office installed.
- Templates and add-on packs carry Office's own version number from this
  release on.
- An installed copy carries the licence texts of the packages built into
  its windows (React, and LiveKit's client with the packages it is built
  from) in THIRD-PARTY-NOTICES.txt beside the program.

## Not in this release

- A meeting holds four people at most, and Live Broadcast and the
  Recorder three guests.
- A group conversation needs 2.27.0 on each person's computer. Someone
  on an older copy does not see the group, and Talk tells you that they
  need an update.
- A meeting of more than four people, held on a Talk Server, is built
  into this copy and not offered yet. The switch for one, and the part
  of Talk's settings for a server you run, show only once Rutba's Talk
  service knows a server that can hold the meeting. Today it knows none.
- A camera on the network is read as H.264 only.
