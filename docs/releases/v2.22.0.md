Rutba Office 2.22.0 brings Live Broadcast, the fourteenth app: going live with several cameras, the screen and the sound, switching between the cameras' shots by hand, by who is speaking, or on a timer. The Recorder it grew out of is rebuilt as one studio in which a screen, a window and a camera are all handled the same way. Every app's menu now has the same order, the AI tools can record a window, and faults found while filming 2.21 are put right.

## Live Broadcast

Going live was the Recorder's Go Live. It is now an app of its own, with
its own tile and its own icon, because a recorder that also streams did
not say what it was for.

- **The studio, live.** The same share, list and scenes as the Recorder,
  streamed to YouTube, Facebook, Twitch or any RTMP server at once, with
  the stream keys kept encrypted on this computer and a copy recorded
  meanwhile if you like. Live Destinations is in the app menu and in its
  settings.
- **A shot for each camera.** With two cameras or more, say one on the
  front and one on the side, or one on each speaker, each camera has a
  shot of its own (Camera 1, Camera 2 and so on, keys 1 to 9) and the
  wide shot, Everyone, puts them side by side.
- **Preview, Cut and Fade.** With Preview on, the shot you pick waits
  beside the one on air, where you can still move, clip and shape what is
  in it. Cut puts it on air at once and Fade dissolves it in over the shot
  before; Space takes it with the transition you chose.
- **Follow the speaker.** Pair each shot with a microphone from the
  shot's own menu (with as many microphones as cameras, they are paired
  in order for you). A voice heard for a second and a half brings its
  shot up; a shot stays at least three seconds; when two speak at once,
  or nobody has for a while, the wide shot goes up. A shot you pick by
  hand takes over from it.
- **Rotate.** A new shot every 3 to 30 seconds, through the shots you
  mark "In the rotation", or through the cameras' shots and the wide one.
- Opened the first time, Live Broadcast starts from the Recorder's
  sources and settings. Anyone who had the Recorder has Live Broadcast
  too, until they turn it off in Choose apps or the installer.

## Recorder

- **One studio, every source the same.** A screen, a window and a camera
  are each shown, clipped, shaped (rectangle, rounded or circle), placed,
  mirrored, started and stopped the same way, from the bar over the share.
  The list on the right holds them with their pictures: those on the share
  first, top first, then those not shown, then the sounds with their
  meters. Drag them, or use Alt with the arrow keys, to change the order.
- **The first line.** Take Picture and Record side by side, Record red
  with its time while it records and saying whether it records video or
  sound; then the Video switch (video, none, a placeholder card) and the
  Sound switch (sound, none, music), then the settings.
- **Pictures at any time.** Take Picture keeps what is shown, or the one
  source chosen, while it records too.
- **Recorder tools for AI apps.** In the Office MCP, recorder_windows lists
  the windows and screens, recorder_start records one into a folder the
  AI tools may make files in, recorder_stop gives back the file, and
  recorder_state says how it is going. AI Tools has a Recorder row, Off
  until set to "Record windows and screens". Going live is never a tool.

## Every app: the app menu

- **One order everywhere.** New, New from Template, Open and Open Recent
  (this app's files first, other apps' in a submenu); Save, Save As,
  Export and Print; the app's own items; then Window, Settings (theme,
  language, digits, profiles, AI Tools), Help (what's new, keyboard
  shortcuts, how-to films, contact, about) and Close Window last.
  Submenus open by hover, by click and by the arrow keys, mirrored for
  right to left languages.

## Fixed

- **Mail in the server's Junk.** Mail a server filed in its Junk folder
  before Rutba Office first fetched it never showed. The server's Junk is
  now fetched from the start, and a server folder opened is asked for its
  mail.
- **Data Files receipts.** A print template made by the wizard showed the
  record's number beside the table's own number, so a receipt read
  "Number 1 Number S1001". It shows the table's own number.
- **Data Files lists on a form.** A long list's matches were drawn behind
  the fields under them, so a click picked the field underneath; and a
  list clicked again kept its words, so typing landed after them. The
  matches now come to the front, and a second click chooses the words.
- **The samples on any day.** The trading, builder and garage samples had
  empty reports on some days, and the trading stock could go below nought.
  Each now has the work its reports print whatever day it is made.
- **Calendar time zones** by the names in use today: Asia/Kolkata,
  Europe/Kyiv, Asia/Ho_Chi_Minh and the rest, where Asia/Calcutta and other
  replaced names were listed.
- **Worksheets.** Home > Cells > Format no longer has a row reading "-".
- **Taskbar icons after an update.** An app open while an update replaced
  its icon showed a blank page on the taskbar. Each tile now reaches
  Windows as a copy named by its content, so the new one is read.
- **Temporary folders.** An embedded object's copy goes when Rutba Office
  ends, and a day-old folder left by a crash is swept at the next start.
- **Templates.** A Custom Office Templates folder not made yet is an empty
  Personal list.
