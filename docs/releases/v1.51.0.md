Bills, invoices and receipts read into the apps: Receipt to Row in Worksheets, an invoice's details on a Mail attachment, and fields and tick boxes in PDF; and Calendar's month shows a day's events again.

## Worksheets

- **Data, From Picture, Receipt or Invoice** reads one or many pictures
  or PDF pages and shows each as a row to check: date, supplier, number,
  subtotal, tax, total and currency. Figures on a page whose items add up
  to its subtotal and whose subtotal and tax add up to its total go in as
  read; the rest are marked to check. Insert puts a row a receipt at the
  selected row, numbers as numbers and dates as dates, as one step Undo
  takes back.

## Mail

- **An invoice's details on its attachment.** When you open a message, a
  PDF or picture attached to it that reads as an invoice or a receipt
  shows who it is from, its total, its due date and its number; each
  shows the place on the page it was read from. It is read only when the
  message is opened, on the recognition threads, and kept with the
  message so it is not read again.

## PDF

- **Find fields.** Recognise Text can also find a scanned invoice's
  details and a form's tick boxes, listed in a Fields pane, and Make
  Check Box Fields turns the tick boxes into real check boxes, ticked as
  they were on paper.
- Fixed: a scanned page's text layer was drawn twice when the zoom
  changed while it was being drawn.

## Text recognition

- Bills and receipts are read in English, Urdu, Arabic, Hindi, Chinese
  and Japanese. A figure goes in unmarked only when the page's sums
  confirm it. Figures printed in Arabic-Indic or Urdu digits are not yet
  read, and a receipt printed in them says so rather than guessing; Urdu
  Nastaliq stays beta.

## Calendar

- **The month shows a day's events again.** Each day shows as many of its
  events as fit, measured and measured again as the window changes size,
  and "+N more" at the foot of a full day opens a list of all of them, as
  Outlook's month does. The 1.49 visual pass had left one event a day,
  with "+N more" beside the date.

## Documents

- At 1300 pixels wide the Home tab keeps every button's name: Find,
  Replace and Select stack under Editing, and Read aloud takes two lines.
