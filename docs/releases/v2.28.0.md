Rutba Office 2.28.0 puts right more than thirty faults in Worksheets and adds the features its training films were missing, adds captions, quick adds and typing with the voice to Video, lets typed words type in on the picture in the Recorder and Live Broadcast, and fixes saved drafts and Archive in Mail, notifications, the paper the print dialog starts on and one installer fault.

## Worksheets

### Faults put right

- **A click selects the cell under the pointer.** In 2.27.0 a click on an
  empty cell could select the cell to its left.
- **What is typed goes in as a number.** A date (9 Nov 2026, 09/11/2026,
  2026-11-09), a time, a percentage, an amount of money and a number with
  commas each go in as a number with its format. The Text format keeps a
  leading 0.
- **A formula that makes a date is shown as a date**, and a loan or
  investment formula is shown as money.
- **A new table is known to formulas at once.** In 2.27.0 a formula such as
  `=[@Qty]*[@Price]` gave #VALUE! until the workbook was opened again.
- **Data Validation acts on what is typed.** A Stop rule keeps the cell as
  it was and says why, and a Warning or Information rule lets the entry in
  with its message. A cell with a list rule shows the arrow that opens its
  choices (Alt+Down too), and a selected cell shows the rule's message. A
  list can come from another sheet or a defined name.
- **Outside borders line the outer edge only**, and Wrap Text makes the row
  taller.
- **Fill Down, Right, Up and Left copy**, as Excel's do. In 2.27.0 they ran
  the fill handle's series, so Mon became Tue. The fill handle keeps its
  series.
- **Format Painter paints.**
- **The Watch Window shows values as the cells do**, £6,651.76 and 5.7%
  rather than the raw numbers, and sits under the grid.
- **Data Table fills from the window**, and Goal Seek says the value it
  found.
- **Cell Styles follow the theme** and are kept in the file as named styles.
- **An empty row that was hidden is shown again by Unhide.**

### New

- **A table's total row**, a table's style changed after it is made, and a
  formula carried into a row that joins the table.
- **Number, text and date filters** in the filter arrow's menu: greater
  than, between, top and bottom, contains, before and after.
- **Conditional formatting**: top and bottom rules, above and below
  average, a rule written as a formula, and rules removed one by one.
- **Rows and columns hidden and shown again**, a tab colour, and a sheet
  copied.
- **The status bar sums a Ctrl selection.** Count, Sum and Average take
  every cell picked, where 2.27.0 took one block.
- **Remove Duplicates** asks which columns to compare and says how many rows
  it took out.
- **Charts can be formatted** from Chart Design: axis titles, data labels,
  the legend and a series colour.
- **PivotTables** sort by label or by value, and Show values as gives a share
  of the grand total, of the row or of the column, or the difference from
  the item before.
- **Sparklines** take their own axis, colours, and markers for the high, low,
  first, last and negative points.
- **A Data Table stays live**, worked out again when its inputs change.
- **The Name Box takes typing.** Type a cell, a range or a name to go
  there, or select cells and type a name to make one. Go To takes a range.
- **Formulas.** XLOOKUP returns a whole row, COUNTIF and SUMIF answer a
  spill range one answer a cell, a sum runs across sheets
  (`=SUM(Week1:Week4!F8)`), and IPMT, PPMT, CUMIPMT and CUMPRINC are added
  for loan schedules.

## Video

- **A caption layer.** Typed or pasted words shown at the foot of the
  frame, revealed one of five ways: typed in, a word at a time, a line at a
  time, karaoke, or all at once. Split into lines makes one caption a line
  or a sentence, one after another. The film has one typing speed for all
  its captions.
- **Quick adds** in the Titles tab: a caption, words on a pill, a QR code,
  a logo and a footer.
- **A caption read aloud types with its voice.** With the voice, each line
  types over the length of its voice and the last letter comes as the voice
  ends. Voice first starts the voice a moment before the typing.
- **Paragraphs and lists can bring their lines in one at a time**, a gap
  apart.
- **The progress bar** takes a colour, a height and pieces, one for each
  marker or each clip. A bar added over the film keeps covering it as the
  film grows.
- **The end card** takes a second line, a logo, a background colour and a
  QR address. The code is drawn only once an address is typed.
- **A new title shrinks to fit the frame.** A title saved before keeps its
  size.
- **Markers are written as chapters** beside the export, a file of
  "0:00 Title" lines for a site such as YouTube.
- Sound fetched from the web is kept in one folder under Music, Rutba
  Recordings, for every project, and no longer beside the project that was
  open.
- The inspector shows what a layer is made of before its timing and place.

## The Recorder and Live Broadcast

- **Typed words can type in on the picture.** The text to speech box has a
  Typewriter switch. Read aloud, each line types in over its voice, with
  the voice or with the voice a moment first. Shown only, the words type at
  the standard speed.
- **The Recorder always draws the typed words.** In 2.27.0 one source that
  failed while being drawn, such as a camera that went away, could stop the
  words being drawn though they were still said.
- **Live Broadcast's stream icons** now work from the streams last chosen,
  and each change is made after the one before. This is to stop a second
  stream that is turned on from stopping the first, which happened once in
  our own checks.

## Talk

- **A call while you are on air is turned away as busy**, and the window
  says who called. A recording does not turn calls away.
- **Open in Live Broadcast asks first** before a meeting moves into the
  studio, and the meeting stays where it is when the studio cannot open.
- **A guest who leaves or drops is named** in the studio window.
- **A message still waiting to be sent offers Send again.**

## Mail

- **A saved draft reaches the server's Drafts folder**, so a phone or
  webmail has it too. The copy on this computer is written first. With no
  connection the draft goes at the next one. If the server will not keep
  it, you are told and the words stay here.
- **Archive on Gmail goes to All Mail.** In 2.27.0 it made a label named
  Archive. Other accounts use their Archive folder as before.

## Notifications

- **Office says why no notification is shown.** File, Notifications reads
  Windows' own switches and says which one stands in the way, with a
  button to Windows' page for it.
- Only the installed copy hands Windows a notification. A copy run from
  anywhere else counts what waits on its icon and shows none.
- The icon by the clock opens its menu on a left click as well as a right
  one. A double click opens Office.

## Printing

- **The print dialog starts on the paper you last chose** in that app, and
  until you choose one, on your region's paper: Letter where the region
  uses it, A4 elsewhere. A workbook that names a paper opens on that.
- A change made in the dialog while a page is being laid out waits for it,
  so the dialog no longer lays out several prints at once.

## Installing

- **A command-line install** is published with each release as
  rutba-office-2.28.0-install.zip. Its script fetches the installer from
  the release, checks its SHA-256 and starts it.
- An install run without administrator rights over what was left of a copy
  for all users now installs for the person. In 2.27.0 Windows could call
  such a run an administrator, and it then ended without installing.

## Not in this release

- Bold or colour on part of a line in a Video paragraph or list.
- Drafts saved by 2.27.0 or earlier stay on the computer until each is
  opened and saved again. An Archive label that 2.27.0 made on a Gmail
  account is left as it is.
- The PDF app and Data Files still print through the system's dialog, and
  the Worksheets page-break view still pages a workbook that names no paper
  as A4.
- Why the first stream stopped once in Live Broadcast is not known for
  certain. The change above covers what our checks point to.
- A camera on the network is read as H.264 only.
