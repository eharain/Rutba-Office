Rutba Office 2.16.1 fixes three things in Data Files' forms: a date and time typed into a form is kept, the words in a form's lists are no longer cut, and a form's list of related records shows names, not numbers. It also brings the newest sample businesses, with their print templates, in the download itself.

## Data Files: forms

- **A date and time is kept.** Typing a time into a form's date and time
  box, such as Signed in on a visitor's record, was refused with a note
  that it takes a date and a time, and nothing was kept. The box gives the
  time to the minute, which is now taken and kept to the second.
- **Lists show their words whole.** The lists on a form, such as a
  visitor's host or a sale's customer, showed only the tops of their
  letters. They now have room for them.
- **Names, not numbers.** A form's list of related records showed a field
  that points at another table as its number, such as a sale's lines with
  their products as 24, 20 and 10. It now shows that record's name.
- **Known by its name.** A record is now known by its Name or Title, or a
  field ending in Name, before a code such as a barcode, wherever a list,
  a report or a print template names it.

## Sample businesses

- **In the download.** Template set 2.16.0, with the sample businesses'
  print templates that say which records they are for and when a part
  shows, comes with Rutba Office instead of arriving at the first update
  check.
