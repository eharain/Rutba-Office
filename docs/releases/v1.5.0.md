Presentation gets PowerPoint's ribbon, and opens the decks people actually send.

1.4.0 gave Worksheets Excel's ribbon; this release does the same for
Presentation — Home, Insert, Draw, Design, Transitions, Animations, Slide
Show, Record, Review, View and Help, every group PowerPoint has, with a PDF
tab where PowerPoint has Acrobat. And, as with Word, the page was measured
against the real thing: a company's own decks (45 files, up to 15 MB)
opened beside the PDFs PowerPoint had exported of them.

## What the slide shows now

- **Custom geometry.** A shape drawn with its own outline — a banner with a
  diagonal cut, a slash, a swoosh — is read into a path and drawn as one,
  scaled onto the shape. Every such shape was a rectangle before.
- **Fit to window.** The slide is drawn at its own size and scaled to fit
  the stage; a 1280-px slide in a 1000-px stage used to run off the right
  edge, logo and all. View → Zoom sets a level, Fit to Window returns.
- **Thumbnails.** The slide pane shows a picture of every slide, and the
  new Slide Sorter shows them all at once.
- **A 15 MB deck opens at once** and stays quick: a slide is parsed once
  per version of its XML, its pictures are held once per session, and a
  keystroke on one slide redraws that slide and nothing else. Before, every
  operation parsed all nineteen slides three times over.

## The Presentation ribbon

- **Home.** New Slide by layout, Duplicate, Delete; the Font group works on
  the selected text box — click one, then Bold, Italic, Underline, size,
  the size steps, font, colour; the Paragraph group's alignments; Arrange
  (delete, nudge).
- **Slide Show.** From Beginning, From Current Slide, Use Presenter View.
- **View.** Normal, **Outline View**, **Slide Sorter**, **Notes Page** (the
  notes editable under the slide), Reading View (the show in the window);
  Ruler, Gridlines, Guides and the notes strip; Zoom levels and Fit to
  Window; Colour, Greyscale and Black and White; New Window; Full Screen.
- **Review.** Speaker Notes. **Help.** Help, Feedback, Show Training,
  What's New, Keyboard Shortcuts. **PDF.** Export as PDF.
- Everything else PowerPoint has — Pictures, Shapes, Tables, Charts, Links,
  Comments, Header & Footer, Themes and variants, every transition and
  animation, Record, ink, master views, Macros — is drawn where PowerPoint
  draws it, disabled, with a title that says why. Transitions and
  animations already in a deck survive a round trip untouched; authoring
  them is not built, and the ribbon says so instead of pretending.

## The ribbon collapses — and Pictures gives the picture the room

Every ribbon now has the chevron Office has at the strip's end (or Ctrl+F1):
it folds the ribbon to its tabs alone, and while folded a tab click peeks
the groups over the content — the content does not move — until a command
is pressed or the pointer goes down elsewhere. Pictures uses it: with a
picture open the ribbon is folded and the stage keeps almost nothing back,
so the picture has the height of the window, the top especially. Opening
the ribbon back out is remembered.

## Honest about the rest

Legacy `.ppt` (the binary format) is not read; Word reads `.doc`, and the
audit now records the gap. Pictures on a slide are the next thing the deck
writer needs, and the highest on its list.
