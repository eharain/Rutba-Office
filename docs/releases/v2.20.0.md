Rutba Office 2.20.0 is two releases in one. Office now installs only the apps you choose, and adds or removes the others later. Profiles keep different people or different work apart on one computer, each with its own passwords, protected by a password if you like. Mail now works at the server: marks, moves, deletes and folders reach your mailbox, and new mail arrives by itself. AI Tools reach mail, calendars, contacts and databases, one thing at a time, and nothing leaves the computer without your press. Documents, workbooks and presentations open and save Strict Open XML, and real documents from public sources open faster and look more as Office made them.

## Only the apps you choose

- **Apps to install.** The installer asks which apps to install. Only the
  apps ticked are written, and only their kinds of file are registered.
  An update keeps the apps you have, and adds any app that is new in
  that version.
- **Add or remove apps** on the launcher adds an app from this version's
  release, or from a folder of app files, and removes one at the next
  start. In a copy installed for everyone it asks for permission once.
- **Each app is a signed pack.** An app's code is in its own pack, signed
  for this release; a pack that is damaged, changed, or from another
  version is refused. The launcher, Choose apps, the Open dialog and AI
  Tools offer only the apps installed, and an app not installed says so in
  plain words instead of failing.
- The portable version carries every app.

## Profiles

- **Profiles side by side,** each in its own folder, with a menu on the
  launcher to switch and manage them. The launcher's badge says whose
  profile is open.
- **An optional password** seals a profile's saved passwords, recent
  files, calendars, contacts, Talk messages and mail. Setting, changing or
  taking off the password is finished at the next start if the computer
  stops halfway, with every message readable.
- **Saved passwords in the profile's own keystore.** Every password a
  profile saves (mail, calendars, contacts, Talk, Data Files' engines and
  sources) is kept in a keystore that opens only in that profile, on this
  computer and this Windows account. A keystore copied elsewhere does not
  open: Office says so once and asks for each password again as it is
  needed. Passwords saved before are moved in once and the old copies
  removed.

## Mail at the server

- **Changes reach the server.** Marks, moves, deletes, junk and the Sent
  copy are made at the server, and a change made offline is sent when the
  server is back.
- **New mail by itself,** as Mail opens, every few minutes, and as it
  arrives where the server tells.
- **Folders** are made, renamed and deleted at the server, and messages
  dragged onto a folder are moved there.
- **One-click unsubscribe** sends its one request from Mail. Out of office
  on Gmail, Outlook, Yahoo and iCloud says that Rutba Office sends the
  replies, and the replies carry the subject they promise.
- **Import mail again.** The same file imported again goes into the
  mailbox made from it and adds only what it did not hold.
- Signature, Out of office and Remove say why they are greyed in All
  accounts.

## Calendar and Contacts

- **Invitations.** Invite offers to send on save, an answer is remembered,
  and the reply's file shows its size.
- **Repeating events.** One occurrence is edited on its own, and a
  reminder stays on a repeating event.
- **Time zones,** with a second zone beside the first, and the small month
  moves the view.
- **Accounts** are named by you and renamed later, and new cards go to the
  account's address book. A setup from before this version keeps new cards
  on this computer until a book is chosen.
- **CSV import** is shown before it comes in, Outlook's older files read
  correctly, and Google's current headings are understood. Greyed buttons
  say why.

## AI Tools

- **One setting per thing.** Each mail account, archive, calendar, address
  book, database and folder has its own setting for AI Tools, Off until
  you choose Read only or more. It is shown in the thing's own app and in
  AI Tools.
- **Nothing leaves without your press.** What an AI app asks to send is
  shown in every window with Send, Keep as draft and Cancel.
- A profile with a password is reached only while it is open.
- The lines to copy into an AI app name a real start file and the profile
  they came from.

## Open formats

- **Strict Open XML.** Documents, workbooks and presentations made in the
  ISO/IEC 29500 Strict form open, and stay Strict when saved. Save as
  Strict Open XML is in each app's menu, and Presentations gains Save as.
- **The Data Files format is published** as an open specification, with a
  checker and sample files.
- **Databases from other programs.** Another program's SQLite database
  opens read only, a newer design is refused in plain words, and what a
  reader does not know is kept.
- **OOXML notes** describe what Word, Excel and PowerPoint files do that
  the standard does not, and what Rutba Office does in each.

## Real documents

Office was checked against 419 public documents from government
statistics offices and open-source test suites, and against a large set
of real business files. Every file that is not damaged or locked with a
password now opens, and comes back unchanged when saved and opened again.

- **Workbooks.** A workbook with a chart sheet opens. A sheet whose
  formulas read whole columns of thousands of rows calculates in seconds,
  not minutes. An .ods styled to the sheet's edge opens in seconds.
  Charts made in Excel are drawn as Excel drew them, a scatter of many
  series and a pie of several among them.
- **Documents.** A long manual opens in seconds, not over a minute.
- **Presentations.** A picture kept as an EMF is drawn, and a picture
  filling a layout's photo placeholder is drawn where the layout puts it,
  cut to its shape. The hexagon is PowerPoint's.
- **Data Files.** Words that start with "=" stay words.
- **Macro-enabled files and templates** (.docm, .dotx, .xlsm, .xltx,
  .pptm, .potx) are saved under their own names, their macros kept.

## Documents, Worksheets and Presentations

- **Documents.** A file whose styles hold only Normal is offered Title and
  Heading 1 to 3, written in when picked. The template row on Home is read
  again when the window has the focus back.
- **Worksheets.** An error cell has a green corner and its tip says why.
  Insert Function shows what each function takes and does, and finds one
  by what it does. The template gallery's search looks among every
  template.
- **Presentations.** New Slide offers the deck's own layouts. A shape is
  drawn by dragging or put down by a click, Shape Fill and Shape Outline
  offer swatches, a second Design Idea takes the first one's place, and
  one undo gives the slide back. Notes are typed under the slide, and
  Clear Narration says what it took away.

## PDF, Pictures, Image and Text recognition

- **PDF.** A file protected with a password asks for it and opens, and
  keeps its protection. A placed signature, tick, date or note is moved by
  dragging and resized by its corners, and Split names the files it wrote.
- **Pictures.** Stars and tags follow a picture renamed or moved on disk,
  the folder shows new files when you come back to it, and the slideshow
  starts in the window, with F11 for the whole screen.
- **Image.** A picture with no layers takes back its changes one at a
  time, Levels' Auto sets only the tones, and the Move tool's click goes
  through a layer's clear parts.
- **Text recognition** reads heavy bold figures and figures on a dark
  ground, keeps a ruled table's rows, and reads a till's VAT codes as
  codes.

## Data Files

- A database's sources and engine links are read only once this computer
  allows them, and a kept password or key goes only to the host or address
  it was typed for.

## Languages

- The new messages are in Urdu, Arabic and Hindi.

## Not in this release

- Gmail and Outlook sign-in, and Google Sheets as a source, wait for the
  sign-in registrations.
- A vector EMF picture shows its frame only, and Documents and Worksheets
  do not draw EMF pictures yet.
- Choosing AI Tools' settings per AI app comes later.
