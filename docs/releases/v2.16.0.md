Rutba Office 2.16.0 opens your own profile again. 2.15.0 started on a new, empty profile, so your mail accounts and settings looked gone. They were not, and 2.16.0 goes back to them as they were. Mail gets an account manager with Remove beside Add account. Talk lets you remove and block contacts, withdraw an invitation and send it again. Data Files' print templates can say which records they are for and when a part shows, and can keep a running balance and group lines with subtotals.

## Your settings and mail accounts

- **Back where they were.** Rutba Office keeps your settings, mail accounts
  and everything else in its profile folder, Rutba Office. 2.15.0 asked for
  that folder a moment too early as it started, before it had named itself,
  and so opened an empty profile under another name. Nothing in your own
  profile was changed or removed, and 2.16.0 opens it again.
- **What 2.15.0 kept.** Anything you set up while 2.15.0 was running is in
  the folder it opened by mistake, @rutba\office-desktop in your
  application data. It is left as it is, not merged and not deleted.
- **Checked from now on.** A test now starts Rutba Office's first step in a
  real Electron and checks the profile it lands in, so this cannot come back
  unnoticed.

## Mail: an account manager

- **Manage accounts.** Beside Add account, Manage accounts lists every
  account with its address, and opens its Signature, its Out of office and
  Remove from the same place.
- **Remove account.** An account's own menu in the side panel offers it too.
  Rutba Office asks first, and you choose whether the mail stored on this
  computer for that account is deleted with it or kept.

## Talk: contacts and invitations

- **Remove or block a contact.** A contact's menu takes them off your
  contacts. Blocking also stops them asking to be in contact again or
  reaching you. Both ask first.
- **Invitations you sent.** Each one shows when it was sent and can be
  withdrawn.
- **Invite again.** A couple of days after an invitation, Invite again
  sends it once more, the same way it went the first time: from your own
  mail account or through Rutba. Until then the button says when it can be
  sent.

## Data Files: print templates that say when and what

- **Only for some records.** A template can say which records it is for,
  for example a delivery note for shipped orders only. A record you choose
  yourself is printed whatever the condition says.
- **Shown only when.** Any part of a template, and any line of its totals,
  can show only when a condition holds: cash tendered and change for cash
  sales, a thank-you for card payments.
- **The lines you want.** The lines can be kept by a condition, such as a
  statement's unpaid invoices, and found by any field of the record, not
  only the one that points at it.
- **A running balance.** A column can add up as it goes, starting from an
  opening balance, and its last value is the closing balance.
- **Grouped with subtotals.** Lines can go under a heading for each value
  of a column, with a subtotal under each group.
- **Formats.** A value can be shown with a format of its own, such as two
  decimal places or a day/month/year date. An empty value prints as nothing.
- **In the designer.** Only for records where, Show only when, Only lines
  where, Group by and Add a running balance are all in the print template
  designer.
