Record with the camera in Presentations, templates of your own, Rotate the picture that saves the file, and four more Power Query transforms.

## Presentations

- **Record with the camera**: recording a show from a deck with cameos
  records the camera too, slide by slide, and puts each slide's recording
  in its cameo's place, as PowerPoint does; in the show it plays with its
  slide instead of the live camera. Record → Reset to Cameo takes a slide's
  recording off and the cameo is live again. A camera that is busy or slow
  to start is waited for, rather than the show being recorded with the
  voice alone.

## Every document app

- **Templates of your own**: Save as Template, in the app menu of
  Documents, Worksheets and Presentations, saves a .dotx, .xltx or .potx
  in the folder Word, Excel and PowerPoint keep templates in (Documents,
  Custom Office Templates), so Office lists the same ones. Home lists them
  under Start something, and choosing one opens a new, untitled document
  made from it, the template left as it was.

## Pictures

- **Rotate the picture**: Ctrl+R, the right-click menu or the bar's button
  turns the picture a quarter clockwise in its file and saves it, as
  Windows Photos does. A JPEG is turned by its orientation tag, its pixels
  untouched; a PNG is redrawn, which loses nothing. Its tile follows. Other
  formats are turned in Image, which Edit opens.

## Worksheets

- **Power Query**: the Query Editor fills a blank cell from the one above
  or below (Fill Down and Fill Up), turns every column but the one picked
  into rows of Attribute and Value (Unpivot Other Columns), joins two
  columns with a separator (Merge Columns), and keeps the first or last
  characters of a text or the part before or after a delimiter (Extract),
  each an applied step.
