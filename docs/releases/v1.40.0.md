Rutba Office in Arabic: every window's words, the frame right to left, and a choice of digits.

## Every app

- **Arabic**: the app menu's Language section offers العربية beside
  English and اردو. Every word the windows show is translated, some 5,350
  messages: the ribbons and their tooltips, the menus, the dialogs and
  panes, the status bars, and what the engines and services say for
  themselves. It is Modern Standard Arabic with the words Microsoft Office
  uses in Arabic, drafted by machine against a glossary of the suite's
  recurring terms (`GLOSSARY-ar.md`, beside the catalogue), and awaits a
  native speaker's review.
- **Six plural forms**: a count reads as Arabic says it, for none, one,
  two, three to ten, eleven to ninety-nine and a hundred or more
  (شريحة واحدة، شريحتان، 3 شرائح، 11 شريحة).
- **Digits**: an Arabic window writes its numbers in Western digits, as
  Office in Arabic does by default; the app menu's new Digits section
  switches to Arabic-Indic digits (١٢٣), in counts and dates alike. An Urdu
  window offers Urdu's own digits (۱۲۳) the same way.
- **The frame right to left**, as Urdu's has been since 1.38.0: the ribbon,
  the panes and the dialogs mirror, while a document's page, a worksheet's
  grid and formula bar and a slide's stage keep the direction their
  document gives them.
- Paper and envelope sizes keep their order in a right-to-left line
  (21 × 29.7 cm, not 29.7 × 21), in Urdu as well.

## Fixed in Urdu

- Source Manager's two Copy buttons point to the list each copies into,
  now that the lists sit mirrored.

## Checked

- The fuzzer damaged 5,400 files at seed 4252, new documents, workbooks and
  decks and every old-format file among the fixtures: 2,551 opened and
  2,849 refused in a sentence, none faulted.
- The catalogue checks are one per language: every message translated,
  every plural form Arabic's rules can choose given, no word left in
  English, and every engine message coming back in its own translation.
- The window checks that open Documents, Worksheets and Presentations in
  Urdu run in Arabic too: the frame right to left, the page, grid and stage
  as their documents have them, typing in Arabic reaching the page, and a
  dialog in Arabic.
