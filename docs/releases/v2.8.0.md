Rutba Office 2.8.0 starts with the Office apps and lets you add the others when you want them. It brings Rutba Talk, the twelfth app, a Video with a library, sound and time tools and starters, a Safety switch in Mail, eleven more templates, and Worksheets opening old spreadsheets and databases: Lotus 1-2-3, dBASE, Access and more.

## Choose your apps

- **The Office apps first.** A new copy starts with Mail, Calendar, Contacts,
  Documents, Worksheets, Presentations, PDF and Pictures. Image, Video,
  Recorder and Talk are there to add from Choose apps whenever you like, and
  so is AI Tools. A copy that ran before keeps every app it had.
- **In the installer.** The installer's Choose apps page has the Office apps
  ticked and the others not, on a first install, and shows your own choice
  after that. It speaks Urdu, Arabic and Hindi on systems in those languages.

## Rutba Talk

- **Messages and calls with your contacts.** Someone becomes a contact once
  both of you have said so. Messages and calls go straight between the two
  computers; a small Rutba service only introduces them. A call shows a
  safety code that both of you can read out to check that nobody is in the
  middle.
- **Relays.** Where two computers cannot reach each other directly, a call
  goes through a relay. You can pick one from a list, let Talk pick, or use
  your own.
- **Not signed in yet.** Rutba sign-in for Talk is still being set up, and
  Talk says so. Until it opens, Talk connects only to a test server.

## Video

- **Library.** A panel beside the preview keeps the media of the project to
  add again, with titles, word art, shapes, icons and ten sound effects made
  on your computer. Shapes, icons and word art are drawn as they will look.
  The library and the properties fold to a rail when you want the room.
- **Starters.** Start a Slideshow, a Tutorial, a Vertical Social video or a
  Montage from the empty preview or the app menu. Each plays at once on
  stand-in pictures, and Replace Media puts your own picture or clip where a
  stand-in was, for the same time.
- **Sound.** Fades as long as you ask, volume in decibels up to +12 dB with
  a limiter, Lower the rest for a voice over music, a crossfade between two
  sounds, and a level meter beside the playhead.
- **Time.** A clip's speed from a quarter to four times, with its sound
  keeping its pitch unless you let it rise and fall. Shift a sound's pitch
  by semitones. Freeze Frame holds the picture at the playhead for two
  seconds, and Reverse plays a clip and its sound backward.
- **Pictures fade too.** A picture clip can fade from black and to black.
- **Preview on Another Screen.** The picture alone in a window of its own,
  full screen on a second display when there is one, in step with the
  editor.

## Old spreadsheets and databases in Worksheets

- **Lotus 1-2-3.** A .wks or .wk1 opens with its labels, numbers, number
  formats, column widths and named ranges, and its formulas work again:
  @SUM, @IF, @VLOOKUP and the rest become the spreadsheet's own, counting
  from where it counts. A formula using something the spreadsheet has no
  word for is kept as its value, and Worksheets says which. A .wk3 or .wk4
  opens with each of its sheets in a tab, its formulas kept as the values
  1-2-3 last worked out.
- **dBASE and FoxPro.** A .dbf opens as a sheet: the field names first, then
  a row a record, dates as dates, numbers with their decimals, and the memo
  fields read from the .dbt or .fpt beside it. Deleted records are left out,
  and Worksheets says how many.
- **Access, read only.** An .mdb or .accdb opens with a sheet for each table.
  Pictures and attached files are left out and counted; forms, reports and
  code are not read. A database with a password is refused with what to do.
- **SYLK and DIF.** The old exchange formats open with their values, and
  SYLK's formulas too.
- **Saved as .xlsx.** Each of these opens as a workbook to save as .xlsx.
  They are in the Open dialog, and Office does not take them over from the
  programs that open them now.

## Mail

- **A Safety switch.** Each message has a Safety switch at the top. With
  Safety off you can copy a link, copy a picture or save it from the
  message. View source shows the message exactly as it came.

## Recorder

- **Go Live keeps up.** When a destination falls behind, the picture's
  bitrate comes down until it catches up, and goes back up after a calm
  spell. The status bar says when it has.
- **Take Picture from anywhere.** Ctrl+Shift+F8 takes a picture while
  Recorder is open, minimised too.

## Templates

- **Eleven more.** A cover letter, a memo, a meeting agenda and a project
  proposal; an expense report, a stock list, a weekly planner and a grade
  book; a quarterly review, a lesson and a portfolio. That makes 27.
- **On the web.** Templates on the web, in the gallery, opens
  office.rutba.io/templates in your browser, where every template can be
  downloaded. The suite itself fetches nothing for it.

## Also

- **Each app loads its own part.** A window loads only what its app needs,
  so the first window opens with less to read.
