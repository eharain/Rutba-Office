Power Query in Worksheets, a thesaurus in all three apps, calendars and contacts kept in step with a CalDAV and CardDAV server, Arabic, Hebrew, Greek and Cyrillic in a document's PDF, Design Ideas worked out on this computer, and a cut pasted that takes every reference with it.

## Worksheets

- **Get & Transform (Power Query)**: Data → From Table/Range opens the
  Power Query Editor on the table or the list round the cell, and From
  Text/CSV on a CSV, TSV or text file, its first row the headings. The
  editor shows the first hundred rows the query would load and builds its
  steps from the column picked: remove it or every other column, rename
  it, give it a type, filter, sort, replace values, split it at a comma,
  a tab, a space or another character, trim it or change its case, group
  by it with a count, a sum, an average, the least or the most, keep the
  top rows, remove duplicates or blank rows, use the first row as headings
  and add an index. The applied steps are listed beside the rows; one
  clicked shows the table as it stood after it, a new step goes in after
  the one picked, and × takes a step away. **Close & Load** writes the
  result as a table on a sheet named after the query.
- **Queries & Connections** lists the workbook's queries with the rows
  each loaded: Refresh runs one again on its source as it is now, Edit
  opens it in the editor, Delete takes the query away and leaves its sheet.
  **Refresh All** runs every query before it refreshes the pivot tables,
  and each refresh is one undo step. **Recent Sources** offers the files
  queries read lately.
- The queries are kept in the workbook, in a part of the suite's own.
  Excel keeps that part and opens the loaded tables as tables; it does not
  read the queries as its own Power Query, which is a binary package this
  suite neither reads nor writes.
- **A cut pasted takes every reference with it**, as Excel's does: a
  formula anywhere in the workbook that names only the moved cells, on
  their sheet or another, names where they went, dollar signs and all, and
  so does a defined name; a range only partly moved stays as it was. The
  cells can be pasted onto another sheet, and what reads them then names
  that sheet. One undo puts every sheet the paste touched back.

## Documents, Worksheets and Presentations

- **Review → Thesaurus** (Shift+F7) opens a pane on the word at the caret,
  the selected word, the active cell's word or the word where the caret was
  in a shape's text: its meanings, each a group of words of like meaning
  under its part of speech, from a thesaurus of the suite's own of some six
  thousand words in British spelling. A word with an ending is looked up by
  the word it was made from; a word clicked is looked up in turn, with Back
  to the one before; Insert puts a word in place of the one in the
  document, in its capitals, as one undo step.

## Documents

- **The PDF speaks Arabic, Hebrew, Greek and Cyrillic.** What the PDF's
  own fonts cannot say is drawn in a TrueType font the computer has whose
  licence allows embedding, subset to the glyphs a page uses, with a
  ToUnicode map so the words copy back out of the PDF. Arabic letters take
  their joined forms, Urdu's and Persian's own among them, with the
  lam-alef ligature; a right-to-left line is put in drawing order by the
  bidirectional algorithm, numbers and Latin words kept as they read and
  brackets mirrored; bold and italic are drawn as Word draws a face it
  lacks. A right-to-left paragraph is laid from the right margin, its
  alignment mirrored and its list label in the right-hand gutter.

## Presentations

- **Design Ideas** (Home and Design → Design Ideas), worked out on this
  computer rather than asked of a service: a pane of layouts the slide's
  own title, words and pictures suit, each drawn as the slide would be:
  the picture on the right or the left beside the words, across the slide
  under a title band, the pictures in a row, the title on a band of the
  theme's accent, an accent bar down the side, the title centred over a
  rule, light words on the theme's dark colour. A click applies one in a
  single undo step; shapes it adds sit behind the words in the theme's
  colours, so a new theme recolours them, and a picture is cropped to its
  new box so it keeps its proportions.

## Calendar and Contacts

- **Accounts on a CalDAV and CardDAV server, kept in step.** Home →
  Accounts adds a server by its address, a user name and a password (an
  app password for iCloud and the like), finds the person's calendars and
  address books and brings them in: each calendar beside the person's own,
  each card among theirs. A sync sends what changed here first, written so
  that an event changed on the server meanwhile is never overwritten
  unseen and the server's copy wins, then fetches only what changed there.
  It runs when an account is added, on Sync, a little after a change made
  here, and every quarter of an hour. Removing an account takes its
  calendars and cards away and touches nothing on the server. Requests go
  only to the server typed, over HTTPS, or plain HTTP to this computer
  alone.
