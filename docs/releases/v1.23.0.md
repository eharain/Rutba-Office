A frame around every page from Design → Page Borders.

## Rutba Word

- **Design → Page Borders.** A menu of boxes — a thin, thick, double or
  dashed line — and none. Written as `w:pgBorders` in the section, after
  the margins where the schema puts it, measured 24 pt in from the page
  edge as Word measures its own; drawn on every sheet in print layout and
  around the flow otherwise; printed by the PDF, where a double border is
  now two lines for paragraphs as well. The button was greyed out.

## Proven, not promised

- Two engine tests write the box, find it after the margins, read the sides
  back, keep them through a save, print the four lines 24 pt in from the
  edges, clear and undo it; then write a heavier double frame measured from
  the text, find the printed line at the margin less the space, and read a
  side another program wrote as nil as no side. Two window checks pick
  Box — thin line and find the frame 32 px in on the sheet, then read the
  saved section (`look`).
