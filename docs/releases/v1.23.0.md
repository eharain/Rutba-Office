Word gets page borders, a watermark and line numbers; a slide gets the Format Painter, the footer band and shadows.

## Rutba Word

- **Design → Page Borders.** A menu of boxes — a thin, thick, double or
  dashed line — and none. Written as `w:pgBorders` in the section, after
  the margins where the schema puts it, measured 24 pt in from the page
  edge as Word measures its own; drawn on every sheet in print layout and
  around the flow otherwise; printed by the PDF, where a double border is
  now two lines for paragraphs as well. The button was greyed out.
- **Design → Watermark.** DRAFT, CONFIDENTIAL, SAMPLE, DO NOT COPY, URGENT,
  any words of your own, or none. Written as Word writes its own — a
  WordArt shape, silver, rising across the page, first in the header — so
  Word shows it; the page and the PDF already drew one that a file brought
  in, and now draw one the suite made. A header's own words stay under it
  and can still be changed. The button was greyed out.
- **Layout → Line Numbers.** None, continuous, restarting on each page, or
  every fifth line: a number beside every line of the body down the left
  margin, as a contract or a manuscript wants. Written as `w:lnNumType`
  in the section after the page borders; drawn beside the lines the page
  actually has, so a paragraph split across pages counts right; printed
  by the PDF beside the lines the paginator laid. The button was greyed
  out.

## Presentation

- **Home → Format Painter.** Click a shape, press it, click another: the
  second takes the first's fill, outline and font — the fill and outline
  into its own properties, the font onto every run of its words — and the
  painter is put down, as PowerPoint's is after one shape. Esc puts it
  down early; the button stays pressed while it is armed. It was greyed
  out.
- **Insert → Header & Footer, Date & Time, Slide Number.** One dialog: the
  date (kept current, or fixed words), the slide number and the footer's
  words, on this slide with Apply or every slide with Apply to All.
  Written as PowerPoint writes them — the three footer placeholders, the
  number and an automatic date as fields — placed where the layout or
  master places them, or along the bottom where PowerPoint's own
  templates put them. The number follows the slide when slides move. All
  three buttons were greyed out.
- **Shape Effects.** A shadow under the selected shape — bottom right,
  below, right, top left, all round — or none, from the ribbon's menu and
  the Format pane. Written after the outline in the shape's own
  properties as PowerPoint writes it, read back, and drawn on the stage
  and in every thumbnail. It was greyed out.

## Proven, not promised

- Two engine tests write the box, find it after the margins, read the sides
  back, keep them through a save, print the four lines 24 pt in from the
  edges, clear and undo it; then write a heavier double frame measured from
  the text, find the printed line at the margin less the space, and read a
  side another program wrote as nil as no side. Two window checks pick
  Box — thin line and find the frame 32 px in on the sheet, then read the
  saved section (`look`).
- Two engine tests put a watermark on a document with no header and find
  the header, its reference and Word's shape made for it, read the words,
  colour and rotation back, keep them through a save, replace, escape,
  remove and undo; then put one over a header with words and change the
  words without losing the shape. Two window checks pick DRAFT and find it
  silver and turned on the page, then read the saved header (`look`).
- Two window checks give a pasted box a fill and an outline, arm the
  Format Painter on it, click the slide's title, and find the look on the
  title and in the saved file (`clip`).
- Two engine tests write the footer, the number and the date onto a slide
  with no layout to place them and find them along the bottom, the number
  a field saying the slide's place and following it when the slide moves,
  new words replacing old in the same placeholder and blanks taking it
  off; then a fixed date as plain words and the number on every slide
  through a save. Five window checks open the dialog from Slide Number
  with the number ticked, type the footer, Apply to All, find both on
  every slide and drawn on the stage, read the saved file, then reopen
  the dialog on slide 2 with its own settings and take the footer off
  that slide alone (`footer`).
- One engine test writes a shadow after the outline, reads it back in
  pixels and degrees, keeps it under a fill write, and turns it off with
  the empty effect list that silences a style's shadow. Two window checks
  put a shadow under the pasted box from the ribbon, find it drawn, and
  read it after the outline in the saved file (`clip`).
- One engine test writes the numbering after the page borders, reads it
  back, prints six numbered lines, changes it to every fifth line with a
  gap in twips, clears and undoes it, and reads Word's own default for a
  file that says nothing about restarting. Two window checks pick
  Continuous and find 1, 2, 3 down the margin with a gap before the text,
  then read the saved section (`look`).
