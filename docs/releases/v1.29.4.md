Track Changes that records what Word records, building blocks, Excel 365's newer formulas, right to left in Documents, a junk filter and rich signatures in Mail, Edit Points, pattern fills and 3-D in Presentations — and the bugs found in 1.29.3 put right.

## Documents

- **Track Changes records formatting.** Bold, a font, a size, a colour, a
  style, alignment, spacing and indents changed while tracking are kept as
  Word keeps them, shown in All Markup with who made them, and accepted or
  rejected one at a time or all at once. So are **Replace All**, **Sort**,
  and paragraph marks — Enter, Backspace and Delete across paragraphs, and
  each line of a paste — each mark drawn as a ¶ underlined or struck through.
- **Right to left.** A paragraph Word marks right to left (w:bidi), or whose
  style does, runs from the right margin with its indents, hang, borders and
  drop cap mirrored. **Home → Paragraph** has Left-to-right and Right-to-left
  buttons; aligning a right-to-left paragraph writes its alignment mirrored,
  as Word does.
- **Building blocks.** Insert → Quick Parts saves the selection to the Quick
  Part or the AutoText gallery under a name, and puts it in again with a
  click — in this document or any other, a heading still a heading, bold
  still bold. The Building Blocks Organizer lists them to put one in or
  delete it.
- **Ctrl+L, Ctrl+E, Ctrl+R and Ctrl+J** align the paragraph, as the buttons'
  tooltips always said; **Justify** shows pressed in a justified paragraph.

## Worksheets

- **Excel 365's newer functions**: LAMBDA, named or called where it is
  written, with MAP, REDUCE, SCAN, BYROW, BYCOL, MAKEARRAY and ISOMITTED;
  XMATCH; TEXTSPLIT, TEXTBEFORE and TEXTAFTER; VSTACK, HSTACK, TOROW, TOCOL,
  WRAPROWS, WRAPCOLS, TAKE, DROP, CHOOSEROWS, CHOOSECOLS and EXPAND; SORTBY;
  RANDARRAY; VALUETOTEXT and ARRAYTOTEXT.
- **PivotTable Fields** stands beside the sheet when the cell is in a pivot:
  tick a field into Rows or Values, move fields between areas, and sum,
  count, average or take the largest or smallest — the pivot laid out again
  at once. Insert → Field List opens it again once closed.
- **Deleting a sheet is one step of undo** — the sheet comes back with
  everything on it.

## Presentations

- **Edit Points.** Home → Edit Shape, or a shape's right-click: drag a point
  and the curves either side bend with it, right-click to delete one or add
  one on the outline, Escape to leave. The shape becomes a path of its own,
  kept in the file as PowerPoint keeps one.
- **Pattern fills** — the gallery of fifty-four, in two colours — and **3-D**:
  a bevel, depth and its colour, and a 3-D rotation, read, drawn and written
  as PowerPoint keeps them, in the Format pane.

## Mail

- **A junk filter.** Home → Junk marks a message junk or not junk, blocks a
  sender, or never blocks one or their domain. **Junk email options**: off,
  low, high or safe lists only; the Safe and Blocked Senders lists; contacts
  trusted. Arriving mail is filed by the lists, the server's own spam
  headers and a filter that learns from what you mark — nothing is sent
  anywhere to decide — and a message in Junk says why, with Not junk beside
  it. At the default (low), an untrained filter files only what the server
  itself marked as spam.
- **Rich-text signatures**: bold, italic, underline, colour, size, a link and
  a picture. A picture in a signature goes as an attachment of the message,
  which every mail reader shows.
- **Insert link** in the composer works, and switching to Plain text and back
  keeps what was typed.

## Files

- **OpenDocument presentations** open as they were made: slides at their own
  size with each drawing where it stood — text boxes, pictures, shapes in
  their fill and outline with their words, lines and tables — and the notes.
  **OpenDocument spreadsheets** keep their column widths, hidden rows and
  columns, and frozen panes.

## Fixed

- Documents: typing in a paragraph holding a change of formatting Word had
  recorded wrote a file Word could not open; so did Accept or Reject on a
  paragraph Word added while tracking.
- Documents: each keystroke in a paragraph holding a tracked deletion wrote
  the deletion again; formatting across one moved it to the paragraph's end;
  editing beside a tracked move brought back the words moved away — a
  paragraph holding a move is now left as it is until the move is accepted
  or rejected.
- Worksheets: typing and then adding, moving, renaming or deleting a sheet
  lost the typing.
- Worksheets: a formula with a function newer than Excel 2007 was saved
  without the prefix Excel needs, so Excel showed #NAME?.
- Mail: moving a message — Archive, Junk, Move to, a rule — lost its
  attachments.
- Mail: rules and automatic replies passed over a message delivered late with
  the date it was written, and handled an older one in its place.
- Mail: a folder made by a move or by arriving mail did not appear until the
  account was opened again.
