Rutba Office 2.14.0 lets Mail's Out of office turn on your mail server's own automatic reply, so it answers with your computer off, draws Calendar's events over several days as one bar across them, and prints whole numbers in Data Files as Access does, a year as 2018.

## Mail: Out of office at your mail server

- **Your mail server sends the replies.** Where the server lets Office set
  it, Out of office turns on the server's own automatic reply, with your
  message, subject, start and end, and turns it off again. Replies go out
  with your computer off. Rutba's own mail server offers it, as do most
  that use Dovecot (ManageSieve).
- **Your filters keep working.** A server runs one set of filters at a
  time. If you have your own, the reply runs first and then your filters,
  and turning the reply off gives your filters back as they were.
- **Chosen for you, said plainly.** The dialog asks your server first and
  chooses it when it can. When it cannot (an imported mailbox, a server
  without automatic replies, an account signed in with Google or
  Microsoft for now), it says why and Rutba Office sends the replies
  itself while it is running, as before. It never does both.
- **Turned on elsewhere.** Opened on another computer, the dialog shows the
  reply the server holds, and the banner's Turn off turns it off at the
  server.
- **Signed in safely.** Office talks to the server over an encrypted
  connection only, with the same sign-in as your mail.

## Calendar: events over several days

- **One bar.** In the month an event over several days is one bar across
  them, in the same row on each day, named where it starts and again where
  a week starts. A day's other events sit below it.
- **Each of its days.** The list of the next thirty days shows it on every
  one of its days, saying which day it is: Day 2 of 3.

## Data Files

- **Whole numbers as Access prints them.** A year prints as 2018, not
  2,018, in print templates and reports. Amounts keep their separators.
- **No more NaN.** A print template's lines column that names a field
  pointing at another table (a student, a product) prints that record's
  name, whatever type the template gives it.
- **Pictures of the samples.** Each sample business has its picture on
  office.rutba.io, taken from the app.
