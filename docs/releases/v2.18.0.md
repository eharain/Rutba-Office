Rutba Office 2.18.0 is a big release for Documents, Worksheets and Data Files. Documents gains Tab as in Word, Modify Style, comments attached to the words they are about, and tracked changes reviewed one at a time. Worksheets gains filter arrows, charts that follow their cells, a Chart Design tab and PivotTable filters, and conditional formatting's Apply works again. Data Files forms can be laid out in Design, subform lines are changed where they stand, and reports choose their totals. A table can now be kept as a copy of an engine's, or read from a CSV file, a spreadsheet, another database or a web address. It also puts right the faults found while making the how-to films, in Data Files, Mail, Pictures, PDF, Image and Presentations.

## Documents

- **The Letter template no longer freezes.** Typing in a line that holds a
  tab no longer adds a tab with each key. A page break no longer grows a
  line break beside it, and a triple-click selects just the line.
- **Tab and Shift+Tab as in Word.** At the start of a list item they move
  it a level in or out. In a table Tab goes to the next cell, and from the
  last cell it adds a row. Anywhere else Tab types a tab, and the keyboard
  stays on the page.
- **Modify Style.** A right-click on a style in the gallery offers Modify
  Style and Update to Match Selection. Modify sets the style's font, size,
  bold, italic, colour and spacing; every paragraph with the style changes,
  and the change is saved with the document.
- **Comments on the words they are about.** A new comment is attached to
  the words selected, or the word at the caret. Those words are marked on
  the page and saved as Word's comment range, and the comment is signed
  with the same name as tracked changes. A paragraph holding a comment can
  now be edited.
- **Tracked changes one at a time.** Next and Previous select each change
  in turn, and Accept or Reject This Change acts on that change only.
  Accept All and Reject All work wherever the caret is.
- **Page numbers on every page.** Page number and Of total in headers and
  footers write Word's PAGE and NUMPAGES fields, so every page, print and
  PDF shows the right number. Tab in the header and footer box types a tab.
- **Markdown kept as written.** A Markdown file opened and saved with no
  edit comes back exactly as it was. A task item shows a check box to click.
- **Save as** is in the Documents app menu, beside Open and Save as Template.

## Worksheets

- **Conditional formatting's Apply works again** for every rule. Not equal
  to and Unique values now apply, and colour scales and data bars (blue,
  green, red) are offered. Data Validation's Apply works again too, with
  List, Whole number, Decimal and Text length.
- **Filter arrows.** A filtered list, or a list given Data, Filter, has an
  arrow in each heading cell. It opens a menu that sorts by the column,
  keeps the values ticked in its list (with a search), or clears the
  column's filter.
- **Charts follow their cells.** A chart redraws as soon as a figure it
  shows is edited, and the saved file keeps the figures it was drawn from.
  A chart inside a group does not follow its cells yet.
- **Chart Design.** Selecting a chart shows a Chart Design tab where its
  title is set or removed. A new chart is made from the selected block when
  there is one. The axes and legend are not on the tab yet.
- **PivotTable.** A new PivotTable is scrolled to with PivotTable Fields
  open beside it. The pane has a Filters area, its own Refresh button, and
  By month for a date field.
- **Save as, and old files.** Save as is in the app menu. Ctrl+S on a file
  opened from WK1, XLS, DBF, MDB or another format Worksheets cannot write
  asks where to save it as a .xlsx, instead of showing an error.
- **Sort by the heading row.** The Sort dialog names the columns from the
  list's heading row, that row is never sorted into the data, and A to Z
  and Z to A say which column they sort by.
- **Tables grow.** A value typed in the row just under a table makes the
  table, and its filter, one row longer.

## Data Files: forms, subforms and reports

- **Form Design.** A form's fields are moved and sized on its grid, by
  dragging or with the arrow keys and Shift. Labels are renamed, and fields
  taken off and put back, all saved with the form.
- **Subform lines.** A subform's values are clicked and changed where they
  stand, and a new line typed at its foot is added to the record shown.
- **Report totals.** A report's Totals chooses Sum, Average, Count or none
  for each number, whole numbers and a query's numbers included.
- **Delete asks first** on a form.

## Data Files: copies and other sources

- **A copy of an engine's table.** A table linked to PostgreSQL or MySQL
  can be kept as a copy in the file. It is worked on with the engine out of
  reach, and caught up with the engine when it is back, or at once with
  Sync now. When a record changed at both ends the engine's version is
  kept, and the one from here is listed beside it, never lost unseen.
- **Tables read from other places.** A table can be read from a CSV file
  or a spreadsheet, from another Data Files database, or from a web address
  that answers with JSON, across its pages. It is read again every 15
  minutes (or 5, or 60, or only when asked), kept in the file as a copy.

## Data Files: the rough edges

- **Dates and amounts on import.** 12/04/2025 is read as 12 April, unless a
  day above 12 says otherwise, and Import says how it read the dates.
  Amounts with a money sign, or with two places throughout, come in as
  Currency, and a CSV's own ID numbers are kept as the key.
- **Workbooks import.** Import takes .xlsx and .ods workbooks: every sheet
  as a table, or the one chosen.
- **Clear refusals.** A type change a value does not fit names the records
  and values, and offers to clear them and make the change. A missing
  related record is named ("Products has no record 99, so Product cannot
  point at it"), and a delete says which records point at the one going.
- **Related fields by name.** The datasheet shows a related field as the
  record it points at: Milk, not 1. A record named by nothing of its own is
  listed by its number and date, such as "Sale 17, 21/10/2026".
- **Queries.** A criterion can name another field (`<= [Reorder Level]`).
  Decimals stands beside Total in the query designer, an average is headed
  "Average of Total", and Avg typed in an expression says how to get one.
  The SQL view keeps its text when it is left, and Save As keeps the query
  in the list with the others.
- **New records and print templates.** The new-record row shows each
  field's default. A form with no print template says how to make one, a
  template's record chooser names its records, and Letterhead says what it
  is for.

## Mail

- **The next message after a delete.** After a delete, archive, junk or
  move, the message below is selected and opened, as in Outlook, instead of
  the list starting again from the first message.
- **Sent appears at once.** Sent shows among the folders as soon as the
  first message is sent, without opening the window again.
- **Import mail names files, not folders.** Import mail names each file it
  found or is reading, not its full path through your own folders.

## Pictures, PDF, Image and Presentations

- **Pictures.** A tile's right-click menu acts on the tile clicked, not the
  picture on show, and works with nothing on show.
- **PDF redactions.** Apply Redactions lists the marked words properly,
  words from two lines apart included, and asks about marks not yet saved
  before the window turns to the redacted copy. Keep It Open leaves the
  original open, with the copy in a window of its own.
- **Image.** Closing a picture whose edits are not exported asks first, and
  so does an Edit in Image window whose picture has not gone back. Export
  offers "{name} edited" beside the original, never the original's own name.
- **Presentations.** The presenter view's Pause pauses the clock only, and
  the show goes on. Reset puts the clock back to 0:00 without going back to
  the first slide.
