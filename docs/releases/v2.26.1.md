Rutba Office 2.26.1 makes added voices work when they are added in one go, adds a default voice, and lets you hear spoken words as they are recorded.

## Voices and languages

- **Default voice.** In Voices and languages, each voice you have added
  has Make default. The default voice is used in Read Aloud, and is the
  one offered first for spoken words in Video, the Recorder and Live
  Broadcast, unless you pick another there. System voice in the Read
  Aloud bar is still a choice of its own, and stands over the default.
  The default reads only the languages it reads: text in another language
  is read by the system voice.

## Recorder and Live Broadcast

- **Hear the voice on this computer.** Beside the words to say, and with
  a title's spoken lines, a box lets you hear the voice as it goes into
  the recording or the stream. It is off until you tick it, because a
  microphone near the speakers picks the voice up a second time: use
  headphones while a microphone is on.

## Fixed

- **Voices that were added and never spoke.** In 2.26.0 the window
  offered Restart as soon as the first small part had arrived, while the
  speech model, about 311 MB, was still coming. Restarting then cut the
  model off, and the voices were listed as added and only the system
  voice read. Restart is now offered once everything set going has
  arrived.
- **What was cut off is brought again.** At a start, Office now brings
  what an addition was still fetching when Office closed, and what a
  voice or language here lacks. If 2.26.0 left you with voices that never
  spoke, this version brings their speech model at its first start once
  the computer is online, and Voices and languages then offers Restart.
  Nothing you removed yourself is brought back.
- **A second voice no longer brings the speech model again.** Each voice
  added in a row fetched the model once more. A part on its way is now
  shown as being added, and is fetched once.
- **A voice uses the speech model you have.** With the smaller model
  added, each new voice fetched the larger one as well. A voice is now
  met by a model already here.
