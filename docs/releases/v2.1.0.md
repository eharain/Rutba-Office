Video exports its own MP4 at twice real time; Image keeps selections, carries masks with their layers and takes typing on the picture; every app passes an accessibility audit and works from the keyboard, with a screen reader and in High Contrast; and updates no longer stop because Rutba Office was still closing.

## Video

- **Export writes the MP4 itself, faster than real time.** The picture and
  the sound are encoded by the computer's own hardware (H.264 and AAC) and
  written to the file as they are made, by a writer of the suite's own. A
  60-second 1080p timeline exports in about 28 seconds on the reference
  laptop. Where a codec is not available the old way remains, by itself.

## Image

- **Selections are kept in the project.** The selection on the canvas, and
  each selection an adjustment layer was made with, are saved in the .rimg
  file and come back with it; Load Selection puts one back.
- **A mask follows its layer.** Scale, turn, Free Transform and Perspective
  carry a layer's mask with it, as Photoshop links them, and Undo takes both
  back together.
- **Words are typed on the picture.** A double-click on a text layer opens
  it in place over the picture, with the caret, selection and typing in any
  language, and a bar for the font, size, bold, italic, alignment and
  colour; each spell of typing is one step for Undo.

## Every app: accessibility

- **An audit of every window.** Every app's window, every ribbon tab, the
  app menu and the main dialogs pass axe-core's checks for WCAG 2.2 at
  levels A and AA with nothing serious or critical: every control named,
  every field labelled, landmarks, contrast, and targets at least 24 pixels.
- **The keyboard alone.** F6 and Shift+F6 move between the ribbon, the
  panes, the document and the status bar, as Office's do; menus follow the
  arrow keys, Home and End, and Escape goes back to what opened them; the
  focus is never left on nothing.
- **A screen reader is told what is in each document**: a document's
  headings, a worksheet's active cell with its address and value, a deck's
  slides by name, Mail's list and the message open, the calendar's dates,
  and a PDF's pages and words.
- **Windows High Contrast.** Every app reads with the system's own colours:
  the chosen, pressed and current items outlined, edges and the focus ring
  drawn, and icons in the system's text colours.
- docs/ACCESSIBILITY.md says, criterion by criterion, what is met and what
  is known not to be.

## Installing and updating

- **An update no longer stops with "Uninstaller error 2".** The installer
  starts the moment Rutba Office begins to quit, and the copy being replaced
  refused to be removed while it was still closing. The installer now tries
  again once a second; after five seconds it asks Rutba Office's windows to
  close, after ten it ends whatever is left of it, and only after fifteen
  does it ask, with Retry. Unsaved work is copied for Recover every minute,
  so a copy closed this way loses under a minute of it at most.
- Releases are now made as drafts and published only once every file is
  up and the update feed has been read back and checked against them, so an
  installed copy can never find a release without its installer.
- Each release now carries a software bill of materials (CycloneDX) listing
  every package and file it ships with its licence and hash;
  docs/REPRODUCIBLE.md says how a build is reproduced.

## Mail

- When a build has no Google or Microsoft sign-in set up, adding a Gmail or
  Outlook.com address now says to use an app password and where to make
  one, in every language the suite speaks.
