Rutba Office 2.0: the version numbers start again at 2, and the review tools are finished in every editor. Presentations gains Redact, Inspect, ink and a slide show you can draw on, and comments with mentions; Worksheets gains Inspect Workbook and Redact, and its cells stay readable in the dark theme. PDF's Find marks the right words on a scan.

## Why 2.0

The 1.x numbers ran past fifty in a few weeks, one minor number for each
release that added anything. 2.0 starts the count again. It is still a
beta, offered as an update like every 1.x release; the release candidate,
code signing and a long-term line come later in the 2.x line.

## Presentations

- **Redact and Inspect Presentation.** Review, Redact marks words on
  slides, in notes and comments, alt text and areas of pictures; Apply
  saves a copy with them taken out of every part of the file. File, Info,
  Check for Issues, Inspect Presentation finds, and removes when asked,
  comments (PowerPoint 365's modern comments included), notes, personal
  information, properties, custom XML, invisible and off-slide objects,
  ink and embedded files.
- **Ink, kept as PowerPoint keeps it.** Pen and highlighter strokes from
  the Draw tab are saved as PowerPoint saves ink, and ink drawn in
  PowerPoint opens.
- **Draw on the slide show.** Ctrl+P, Ctrl+I and Ctrl+E choose the pen,
  the highlighter and the eraser during a show, with a small bar for them,
  and leaving the show asks whether to keep the ink annotations, as
  PowerPoint does.
- **Comments as PowerPoint 365 keeps them.** An @ and a name in a comment
  or reply mentions that person, shown in the accent colour and offered as
  you type; Edit comment and Edit reply change your own words in place;
  Resolve and Reopen are saved with the deck.
- Fixed: Ctrl+P during a slide show also opened Print; Redact on a slide
  marked a picture's hit box rather than the picture itself.

## Worksheets

- **Inspect Workbook and Redact.** File, Info, Check for Issues, Inspect
  Workbook finds, and removes when asked, comments and notes, personal
  information, properties, custom XML, headers and footers, hidden sheets,
  rows, columns and names, and invisible content. Review, Redact marks words
  and numbers (a search, email, telephone and card patterns, or a pattern
  of your own) and saves a copy with black bars in their place, read back
  to prove no part of the file still holds them.
- **The dark theme keeps a cell readable.** A cell's own fill stays as the
  file has it, as Excel keeps it, and words in automatic colour take
  whichever ink reads on that fill; colours the file gives words are kept
  on a fill and lifted where there is none.

## PDF

- **Find marks the right word on a scan.** After Recognise Text, Find's mark
  sits on the word it found at every zoom, on scanned and typed pages, in
  English, Urdu and Arabic. Each recognised word is now its own run in the
  invisible layer, and its box is drawn in to its ink.

## Every app

- **The dark theme reads everywhere,** measured rather than judged: words
  in an app's accent colour are lifted where they were too dark to read,
  white words on accent fills sit on a deeper fill, the quietest grey is a
  step lighter, and a message's own colours in Mail are adjusted by the
  same rule as a workbook's cells, so dark text on a dark page does not
  happen.

## Not yet

- A Compare review of a deck lasts for the session; it is not saved with
  the deck to finish later.
- Mentions are saved as text, not as PowerPoint's mention records.
- Pictures in a workbook cannot be redacted by area.
- A document's block-level content controls are read and shown but not
  edited.
