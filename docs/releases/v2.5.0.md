Rutba Office 2.5.0 adds Rutba Recorder, an eleventh app that records your screen, camera and voice as one finished video and can stream it live to YouTube, Facebook, Twitch or any RTMP server. Video now plays a video file before it edits it and records a voice on its own, and Mail opens a draft to be finished and a message in a window of its own.

## Rutba Recorder

- **An app of its own.** Recorder has its own tile in the launcher and its
  own window: the scenes on the left, the picture as it is recorded in the
  middle, and the sources on the right.
- **Scenes.** The screen with the camera in a corner, the screen alone, the
  camera alone, the two side by side, and a Be right back card. Change the
  scene at any time, while it records too.
- **Sources.** A screen or a window; the camera, round, rounded or square,
  in the corner you choose, mirrored or not; the microphone and the
  computer's own sound, each with its level.
- **A title and a logo.** A name and a line under it across the foot of the
  picture, and your logo in a corner.
- **One finished file.** Record makes one video at 720p or 1080p and 24, 30
  or 60 frames a second, written as it goes, so a recording cut short by a
  crash still plays. Pause holds it, and a key stops it from anywhere. When
  it stops, Play it, edit it in Video, or show it in its folder.

## Go Live

- **Stream to several places at once.** YouTube, Facebook and Twitch, with
  their servers filled in, or any RTMP server. Paste the stream key the
  platform gives you once.
- **Your keys stay safe.** A stream key is kept encrypted by Windows on this
  computer and is sent only to the server it belongs to. The window shows
  only its last four characters.
- **Made for live.** The picture is encoded on the graphics chip where there
  is one, at a steady bitrate with a key frame every two seconds, as the
  platforms ask. A destination that drops is tried again by itself. Each
  destination's state shows under the picture, with LIVE on it.
- **A copy on this computer.** While it streams, Recorder can record a copy
  at the same time, on by default.
- **Nothing connects until you press Go Live,** and only to the servers you
  set up. The box says how much upload the streams need.

## Video

- **Play first.** A video or a sound file opened in Video plays in a player:
  speed, repeat, captions from a subtitle file of the same name beside it,
  picture in picture, full screen, and the files before and after it in the
  folder. Space, J, K, L, the arrows, M, F and C work as in other players.
  Edit Video puts it on the timeline. A new video and a .rvid project open
  in the editor as before.
- **Record Voice.** The recorder records your voice alone, with no picture,
  and Video puts it on a sound track at the playhead. Record Voice is on the
  Home and Insert tabs.

## Mail

- **A message in a window of its own.** Double-click a message in the list
  to open it in its own window, with the ribbon to answer, file or delete
  it.
- **Drafts open to be finished.** A draft opens in the message editor with
  its recipients, subject, formatting and attachments, instead of in the
  reading pane. Saved again, it replaces itself; sent, it leaves Drafts.
  Save draft now keeps Cc, Bcc, the formatting and the attachments.
