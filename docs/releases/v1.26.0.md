Slides gathered into sections, and a hidden one left out of the show, in
Presentation; text effects, bookmarks, and Delete and Paste over a selection
in Rutba Word; the zoom slider scaling the page alone, never the window, in
Word, Worksheets and Mail; a message written back to an mbox keeps its
bytes.

## Presentation

- **Home → Section.** The button now opens a menu — Add Section, Rename
  Section…, Remove Section, Remove All Sections — as PowerPoint's does.
  Add Section starts a section at the selected slide and asks for its
  name at once, a "Rename section" dialog prefilled with "Untitled
  Section"; a deck with no sections first gets a "Default Section" for
  the slides before it, as PowerPoint does, since every slide of a
  sectioned deck is in some section, and a slide that already starts a
  section starts it still. Rename Section renames the section the
  current slide is in; Remove Section takes it away and its slides join
  the section before it, the first section's joining the one after; the
  last section standing takes the list with it; Remove All Sections
  takes every section out, the slides staying. Sections are drawn as
  headings above their first slide in the thumbnail strip and in Slide
  Sorter view, the name with the slide count beside it, and a
  double-click on a heading renames it. In the file the sections are
  PowerPoint 2010's `p14:sectionLst` in an extension list on the
  presentation part, written as PowerPoint writes it — one `p:ext` with
  PowerPoint's section-list URI, the p14 namespace on the list, each
  section a name, a GUID and its slide ids; a file with an extension
  list of its own keeps what is in it, and a list PowerPoint wrote is
  read as it comes, its GUIDs kept. Adding, duplicating, moving or
  deleting a slide keeps the sections in step: a new slide joins the
  section of the slide before it, a moved slide belongs to the section
  it lands in, a deleted slide leaves its section, and a section left
  with no slides stays, as PowerPoint keeps one, drawn after the last
  slide so it is not lost. The button was greyed
  out ("Sections are a presentation-part list the engine does not write
  yet").
- **Slide Show → Hide Slide.** The button now marks the slide `show="0"` on
  its own root tag, as PowerPoint does, and takes the attribute off again
  to show it. The thumbnail strip and Slide Sorter still draw a hidden
  slide, dimmed with its number struck through; printing still prints it;
  only the show steps over it — from the keyboard, a click, the presenter
  window, and Home and End — and a show never opens on a hidden slide. The
  button was greyed out.

## Rutba Word

- **Home → Text Effects.** The "A" button opens a menu — Outline, Shadow, a
  glow in gold, blue, green or red, No glow, Clear effects — on the
  selected words. An outline and a shadow are the toggles Word has always
  had (`w:outline`, `w:shadow`, in schema order after strike and before
  the colour); a glow is Word 2010's `w14:glow`, its radius in EMU and its
  colour, at the end of the run's properties. On the page an outline is
  hollow letters, a shadow a soft dark offset and a glow a coloured halo;
  the format painter carries all three. In print an outline is stroked
  (the PDF writer gained a stroke render mode for text) and a shadow is
  the word drawn once more offset in grey; a glow is not drawn — a soft
  blur is not a thing this writer does. The button was greyed out.
- **Insert → Bookmark.** A dialog lists the document's bookmarks and adds,
  deletes or goes to one: a name as Word allows it (letters, digits and
  underscores, starting with a letter, up to forty), the same name again
  replacing the old, the marks written as `w:bookmarkStart` after the
  paragraph's properties and `w:bookmarkEnd` before its close, over the
  selected paragraphs or the caret's. A bookmarked paragraph stays
  editable — typing, Enter and Backspace keep the bookmark — where before
  it made the paragraph read-only; a mark Word placed mid-paragraph widens
  to the paragraph's start on the first edit, the honest cost of keeping
  the paragraph editable. Go To is a cursor move and does not mark the
  file changed. Cross-reference stays greyed: it is a REF field, not
  written yet. The button was greyed out.
- **Delete and Paste act on a selection that ends on the page itself.** A
  selection dragged into the margin, a triple-click or Ctrl+A ends on the
  page element with a child index, not inside any paragraph, and the
  window read that as no position at all: the engine never learnt the
  range, so Delete took one character and a paste landed at the old caret
  with the selected words untouched. Such an end is now, since 2026-09-24,
  the start of the first paragraph at or after that index, or the end of
  the last one before it, so the range reaches the engine as the other
  selections do and Delete, Backspace, typing and a paste all replace it.

## Everywhere

- **The zoom slider scales the page, not the window.** In Rutba Word and
  Worksheets, and the In and Out buttons in Mail, zoom used to scale the
  whole window — the ribbon, the status bar and the slider itself grew
  under the pointer, which made the slider unusable. Since 2026-09-24, Word
  scales the page with CSS zoom on the page element, Worksheets the grid's
  scroll container, and Mail the message in the reading pane; the window's
  chrome stays put and its own zoom factor is reset to 100% once on
  opening, in case an earlier build left it zoomed. Page Width and One
  Page fit the scroll area rather than the window. Presentation already
  scaled its stage alone.

## Mail

- **A message written back to an mbox keeps its bytes.** Writing an
  mbox went through `TextDecoder('latin1')`, which by the encoding
  standard is Windows-1252: it reads the bytes 0x80–0x9F as the euro
  sign, the curly quotes and their neighbours, whose code points do not
  come back to the byte they were, so an em dash in a UTF-8 message (E2
  80 94) went out as E2 AC 1D and read back as mojibake. The reader and
  the writer now go byte for byte. A Subject line carrying raw UTF-8
  (rather than an encoded word) is now read as UTF-8 when it is valid
  UTF-8.

## Proven, not promised

- Three engine tests in `tests/deck-sections.test.js`: one adds a
  section before a slide, the slides before it falling into a Default
  Section, checks the XML written matches PowerPoint's form
  byte-for-byte at the end of the presentation part, reads it back
  through a save, refuses to double a section a slide already starts,
  splits a section, renames it (trimmed, an XML-hostile name escaped, a
  blank name becoming "Untitled Section"), removes one (folding its
  slides into the section before, or after for the first), removes the
  last one and finds the extLst gone, and removes them all; a second
  inserts, duplicates, moves (down, up, to the very front) and removes
  slides and finds the sections kept in step, an emptied section
  written as `<p14:sldIdLst/>`, and a presentation part with another
  extension already in its extLst keeping it before and after; a third
  reads a list written the way PowerPoint writes it, sets aside a slide
  id not in the show, gives an unlisted slide to the section before it
  on the next edit, keeps PowerPoint's own GUID, and adds a section at
  an unlisted slide. Five window checks in the new `sections` block
  (`RUTBA_VERIFY_ONLY=sections`): Home → Section → Add Section on the
  second slide starts a section there with the first slide in a Default
  Section and the name asked for; the typed name is the heading drawn
  in the strip with the slide count; the saved file carries the list as
  PowerPoint writes it; Remove Section folds it back and Remove All
  Sections takes the list out of the strip and the file; and the window
  reports nothing.
- The engine test "a body with characters outside ASCII survives a
  write and a read" in `tests/mailbox.test.js`, which failed before
  this release, now passes: writing and reading an mbox go byte for
  byte, an em dash and a euro sign in a UTF-8 message coming back as
  themselves.
- Four engine tests in `tests/deck-hidden.test.js`: the hidden attribute
  written and taken off with the rest of the slide byte-identical,
  survives a save, hiding twice is no change, a root already saying
  `show="1"` is corrected, a duplicate of a hidden slide is hidden, and a
  bad index throws. Six window checks in the new `hidden` block
  (`RUTBA_VERIFY_ONLY=hidden`) press the button, read the strip, the
  saved file and the show's stepping.
- Eight engine tests in `tests/word-effects.test.js` write and read the
  three effects, hold their schema order, read a file that declares
  `w14` itself, keep them through a save, drop them with Clear
  formatting and report them at the caret. Six window checks in the new
  `effects` block (`RUTBA_VERIFY_ONLY=effects`) put each on a selected
  word from the menu, read the span's stroke and shadow, the saved
  file's elements, and take them off.
- Eleven engine tests in `tests/word-bookmarks.test.js` write and read a
  two-paragraph bookmark, number a second, replace by name, refuse bad
  names, remove, edit, split and merge the bookmarked paragraph with the
  marks kept, skip Word's own `_GoBack`, read a mid-paragraph mark, go
  to and undo. Six window checks in the new `bookmarks` block
  (`RUTBA_VERIFY_ONLY=bookmarks`) add one from the dialog, read the
  model, the saved file, Go To and Delete.
- Six window checks in the new `zoom` block (`RUTBA_VERIFY_ONLY=zoom`)
  set the slider to 150% in Word and Worksheets and read the page and
  grid half as wide again, the ribbon and the slider unchanged, the
  window's own factor still at 1, the pages still falling where they
  did, a press on a cell at its zoomed place selecting it, and the
  percentage putting it back.
- Three editing checks under `npm run verify:edit`: a selection from
  inside the first paragraph to past the second reaches the engine as a
  range, Delete takes the words and the paragraph mark between them, and
  a paste over a selected paragraph replaces it.
