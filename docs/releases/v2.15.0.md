Rutba Office 2.15.0 keeps its templates up to date on its own, and brings them to what was made from them: a database made from one of Data Files' templates or samples is offered the newer design when one arrives, its records and your own changes kept. Data Files' tables and queries can be searched and filtered alike, saved queries open with their records, the side panel takes pinned favourites, and every template links to its page online.

## Templates that keep up to date

- **Fetched with the update check.** The same check that looks for a new
  Rutba Office looks for a newer set of templates: the gallery's documents,
  workbooks and presentations, and Data Files' ready-made databases and
  sample businesses. Turning automatic updates off turns this off too.
- **Signed, then checked.** A set is used only when Rutba's signature on it
  is good and the download is exactly the one signed. Anything else is not
  kept, and Office goes on with the set it has.
- **From the next start.** A new set is kept beside your profile and used
  the next time Rutba Office starts, so nothing changes under an open
  window. The template gallery says when one is ready.
- **Only when Office is new enough.** A set made for a newer Rutba Office
  is not used: the gallery says which version it needs, and updating
  Office brings it.

## Data Files: a newer design for what you made

- **Your database remembers.** A database made from a ready-made one or a
  sample business keeps which it came from, from which set of templates.
- **Offered, never forced.** When the set in use has a newer design of it,
  the database says so as it opens: how many changes to its tables,
  queries, forms, reports and print templates. Update the design brings
  them in; Not now leaves it as it is.
- **Your records and your changes stay.** New tables and fields are added
  and nothing is taken away. A query, form, report or print template you
  never changed is replaced by the newer one; one you changed keeps your
  version, with the newer one beside it, named (updated).

## Data Files: search and filter

- **The same for tables and queries.** Above a table's records and a
  query's results: a search box for words in any column, and a filter on
  every column's heading.
- **By the column's type.** Dates and numbers are filtered by a range,
  from and to, either end left open; words by Contains, Starts with or Is
  exactly; yes/no by Yes or No; and any column by Empty or Not empty.
- **By selection.** Right-click a cell for Only this value or All but this
  value, as in Access.
- **Chips.** Each filter shows as a chip with its cross; Clear all takes
  them off. The count says how many records were found.
- **Quick on large ones.** A query's results are now read a page at a
  time, sorted, searched and filtered in the database, as a table's are.

## Data Files: queries open with their records

- **Run first.** Opening a saved query shows its records and how many, with
  Run again and a clear Edit query button for its design.
- **Back to the results.** The design keeps Run for trying a change, and
  Show results returns to the records.

## Data Files: pinned favourites

- **Pin what you use.** Every table, query, form, report and print template
  has a pin, and Pin to the top in its menu. Pinned items are listed first
  in the side panel, above Tables, in the order you pinned them.
- **Fold the rest.** Each section of the side panel folds away from its
  heading. Pins and folds are kept in the database, so it opens as you
  left it.

## Templates link to their pages

- **From the lists.** Each template in the gallery, and each ready-made
  database and sample business on Data Files' start screen, has a small
  globe that opens its page on office.rutba.io.
- **From what you made.** A database made from one has About this database
  online at the foot of its side panel; a document, workbook or
  presentation made from a gallery template has a link to its template's
  page in the status bar.
