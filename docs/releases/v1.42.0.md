Rutba Office in Hindi, kashida for Arabic, and a week that starts where the language starts it.

## Every app

- **Hindi**: the app menu's Language section offers हिन्दी beside English,
  اردو and العربية. Every word the windows show is translated, some 5,360
  messages, in standard Hindi in Devanagari with the words Microsoft Office
  uses in Hindi, drafted by machine against a glossary of the suite's
  recurring terms (`GLOSSARY-hi.md`, beside the catalogue), and awaiting a
  native speaker's review. Counts follow Hindi's rules, numbers are grouped
  as India groups them (12,34,567), and the Digits section offers
  Devanagari digits (१२३). The frame stays left to right.
- **The installer** opens in Hindi on a Hindi Windows, as it already did in
  Arabic, Chinese, Japanese and the other languages it ships with. Windows'
  installer maker has no Urdu, so an Urdu system's installer stays English.

## Documents

- **Kashida**: Word's three Arabic justifications, Justify Low, Medium and
  High, are kept in the file and offered beside Justify for a right-to-left
  paragraph. Printed and saved as PDF, such a paragraph's lines are filled
  by lengthening the joins between letters with tatweel, as Arabic is
  justified, a third, two thirds or all of each line's slack by the level
  chosen, the rest by the spaces. A kashida goes only where two letters
  join, never inside lam-alef, a word's last join first. On screen the
  paragraph is justified by its spaces, since the window's text engine
  cannot stretch letters.

## Calendar

- **The week starts on the language's own first day**: Saturday in Arabic,
  Sunday in Urdu and Hindi, Monday in English as before.
