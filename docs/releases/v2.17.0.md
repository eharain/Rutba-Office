Rutba Office 2.17.0 brings people and roles to Data Files, and links its tables to a database engine. A database can be protected so that each person signs in and sees and changes only what their role allows. A table can be linked live to PostgreSQL or MySQL, the engine keeping the records for every computer that links to it. Forms name the records they point at, and a list too long to hold is a box you search as you type.

## Data Files: people, roles and protection

- **Protect this database.** From the side panel. The person who protects
  it becomes its Admin, who may do everything, and it asks who you are as
  it opens from then on.
- **People and roles.** The Admin adds people, each with a role and a
  password of their own. A role may find each table hidden, read only,
  open to adding, changing, or deleting too, and may see only the records
  its person added. A field can be hidden or read only, and queries,
  forms, reports and print templates shown or hidden.
- **See as.** The Admin sees the database as a role would, before anyone
  relies on it, and goes back with one click.
- **Held beneath everything.** A query, a report, a print template and a
  list read only what the role may see. Who added, changed and deleted
  each record is kept.
- **Change my password, Sign out.** From the side panel. A mail merge in
  Documents asks for a protected database to be opened in Data Files.
- **The file itself.** It is not encrypted: the roles hold in Rutba
  Office, and Protect this database says plainly that anyone who copies
  the file can read it with another program.

## Data Files: tables linked to a database engine

- **Link to a database engine.** PostgreSQL, MySQL or MariaDB: an address,
  a database and an account, then the tables to link. Microsoft SQL Server
  is listed as coming.
- **The engine keeps the records.** Each change is saved straight to it,
  and every computer linked to the same engine works on the same records.
  The file keeps only how to reach the engine; the password is kept in
  this computer's protected store, never in the file.
- **Live.** A linked table on screen is read again every 15 seconds, and
  changes only when the engine's records did, never while you type in a
  cell. Read again reads it at once.
- **As any table.** Queries join a linked table with your own, and
  reports, print templates and lists read it. Its design is the engine's.
  An engine out of reach says why, and the rest of the database opens.

## Data Files: forms that name what they point at

- **Search a long list.** A list with more choices than it can hold, such
  as a sale line's sale among thousands, is a box you type in to find one,
  showing the record it points at.
- **Records known by their names.** A field that points at another table
  shows that record's words: a sale by its receipt number, an appointment
  by its time and patient, not by a field that repeats. Lists, subforms,
  reports and print templates all name it the same way.
- **Everything 2.16.1 fixed.** Dates and times typed into a form are kept,
  lists show their words whole, labels keep their hyphens, and queries
  round their answers.
