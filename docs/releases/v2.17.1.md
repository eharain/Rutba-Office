Rutba Office 2.17.1 fixes how Data Files' print templates name a related record. Since 2.17.0 they printed it with all the words its lists find it by, so a payslip read "EMP-0143 Camille Sharma" beside the employee number, and a visitor pass "Ivan Cole Hollybrook Services 09/01/2026". On paper a record is called by its name again.

## Data Files: print templates

- **Called by its name.** A field on a print template that points at
  another table, such as a leave request's employee or a receipt's client,
  prints that record's name: Camille Sharma, not EMP-0143 Camille Sharma;
  Ivan Cole, not Ivan Cole Hollybrook Services 09/01/2026. The same goes
  for a name written in a template's text, such as Dear {Tenant}.
- **A name before a code.** A record its lists know by a code, such as a
  landlord by the account CL-0004 or a tenant by TEN-0017, prints by the
  person's name when it has one: "Dear Zara Tanaka", not "Dear TEN-0017".
- **A code when there is no name.** A record with no name, such as an
  invoice or a sale, still prints by what its lists show, such as its
  invoice number.
- **Lists unchanged.** A form's lists, subforms and search boxes still find
  a record by all its words, so two people of the same name can be told
  apart there.
