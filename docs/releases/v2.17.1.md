Rutba Office 2.17.1 fixes how Data Files' print templates name a related record, keeps Mail's list in place when a message is deleted, and gives Data Files' side panel more room. Since 2.17.0 print templates printed a related record with all the words its lists find it by, so a payslip read "EMP-0143 Camille Sharma" beside the employee number, and a visitor pass "Ivan Cole Hollybrook Services 09/01/2026". On paper a record is called by its name again.

## Data Files: print templates

- **Called by its name.** A field on a print template that points at
  another table, such as a leave request's employee or a receipt's client,
  prints that record's name: Camille Sharma, not EMP-0143 Camille Sharma;
  Ivan Cole, not Ivan Cole Hollybrook Services 09/01/2026. The same goes
  for a name written in a template's text, such as Dear {Tenant}.
- **A name before a code.** A record its lists know by a code, such as a
  landlord by the account CL-0004 or a tenant by TEN-0017, prints by the
  person's name when it has one: "Dear Zara Tanaka", not "Dear TEN-0017".
- **A code when there is no name.** A record with no name, such as an
  invoice or a sale, still prints by what its lists show, such as its
  invoice number.
- **Lists unchanged.** A form's lists, subforms and search boxes still find
  a record by all its words, so two people of the same name can be told
  apart there.

## Mail

- **Deleted, and the list stays.** Deleting a message, or moving it to
  Archive, Junk or another folder, took the whole list away and drew it
  again, back at the top. Now that message leaves the list and the rest
  stay where they were, in a folder and in All accounts alike.

## Data Files: the side panel

- **Room for your tables.** Import, Relationships, SQL, Link to a
  database engine, Protect this database and the rest no longer stand at
  the foot of the side panel over the list; they come after Print
  templates and scroll with it.
- **About this database online, by its name.** A database with a page
  online, such as a sample business, shows a small globe beside its name
  at the top of the window, which opens that page.
