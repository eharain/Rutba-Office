Rutba Office 2.23.0 splits the Recorder and Live Broadcast into two panels, Set up and Record or Live, with named setups you can pass to someone else. Live Broadcast goes to ten kinds of destination, wide and tall at once, each turned on or off by its own icon. Home shows your apps as two sets, Office and Studio, and Video can make a file to fit a size limit.

## Recorder and Live Broadcast

Both apps now open on two panels. Set up is where the scenes are made and
Record or Live is where they are used, so the window you work in while
recording is no longer the one you arrange things in.

- **Set up.** Sources on the left, the frame in the middle, properties on
  the right and the scenes underneath. Sources snap to the edges, the
  centre and each other, and Alt drags freely. Place, size and shape can
  be typed.
- **Named setups.** What you arrange is a setup, saved as it changes. Keep
  one for a webinar and another for a podcast, and move between them from
  the top of the window.
- **Pass a setup on.** A setup saves as a `.rstudio` file with its pictures
  and music. Stream keys are never put in it. When a setup opens, its
  sources are found again by what they are, and one that has gone says Not
  found and offers Choose.
- **Record.** The picture large, the scenes as live tiles with their
  numbers, and one bar at the foot with the microphone, computer sound,
  camera and Record. After Stop, a card says what was saved.
- **Back soon and Sound only** are scenes of their own. They replace the
  Video switch with its None and Placeholder choices.
- **Settings** now says what belongs to this setup and what belongs to
  every setup.
- **Video and Presentations record through this window too.** The scenes,
  meters, Take Picture and global keys are the same everywhere. Each
  source can be kept as a file of its own. The old recording panel is gone,
  and Clip replaces the Region drawing. The pointer is always tracked when
  recording into Video or Presentations, and its layer can be deleted in
  Video, so the Pointer and clicks switch has gone.

## Live Broadcast

- **My streams** is a list of what you have set up, with no addresses or
  keys on show. A key is only ever shown by its last four characters.
- **Ten kinds of destination:** YouTube, Facebook, Twitch, TikTok,
  Instagram, LinkedIn, X, Kick, Rumble or any RTMP address.
- **An icon for each stream,** above the picture. Turn one on or off at any
  moment, before or during the broadcast, and the others carry on.
- **One button.** Go live becomes End broadcast, which asks first. Turn
  every icon off and you are OFFLINE with the broadcast still open and a
  copy still recording, so you can take a break and come back.
- **Wide and tall.** Each is your choice, and with both on, both go out at
  once from encoders of their own at their own sizes. Set up has Wide and
  Tall over the frame, and a tall layout is kept for its scene. Keep a copy
  can record either shape.
- **Preview first,** when you want it: changes wait in Next until you press
  Take. Going straight on air stays the default.
- **A web page as a source.** Add an https address and it is drawn out of
  sight in a locked-down window of its own. A strip under the picture flips
  it with Previous and Next, or with a clicker's Page Up and Page Down. A
  page that stops answering shows Not available, never its error page.

None of the ten destinations has been tried on a real account yet. Each
was tested against an RTMP server on the same computer, so what a service
accepts is still to be found out on that service. TikTok decides which
accounts can stream this way.

## Video

- **Every destination is a card** with the size it will come to for this
  project.
- **Compact** (1080p at about 4 Mb/s) and **Small** (720p at about 2 Mb/s)
  make smaller files.
- **Fit under a size** takes 50 MB, 100 MB, 287 MB or a number you type. It
  works the bitrate out from the project's length and the sound's share and
  aims about 8% under. It then reads the size of the file it wrote, and if
  that is still over it encodes again lower and tells you. If the size
  leaves too little for a clear picture at 1080p, it says so and offers
  720p.
- A picture so detailed that the encoder will go no lower is handed over
  with a sentence saying it is still too big. The sizes on the cards are
  worked out from the bitrate, so hard material can come out larger, and
  only Fit checks the file. A limit's MB here is 1,000,000 bytes, so
  Explorer shows a slightly smaller number.

## Home

- **Two sets, side by side.** Office holds Mail, Calendar, Contacts, Talk,
  Documents, Worksheets, Presentations, PDF and Data Files. Studio holds
  Pictures, Image, Video, Recorder and Live Broadcast. Each has its own
  Start something row, and on a narrow window they stack with Office first.
- The same two names, in the same order, appear in Choose apps, the
  installer, the Window menu and the macOS app list.

## Profiles

- **Switching keeps you in the same app.** Choosing another profile from an
  app window's profile menu opens that app in that profile, and brings
  forward a window of it if one is open. All apps in that profile is there
  as an extra item.
- A profile with a password asks for it first, then opens the app. If the
  app is turned off in that profile, its Home opens and says so, with
  Choose apps beside the notice.

## Mail

- **Sign in with Google for Gmail.** While Google reviews the app, it only
  lets in the accounts added as testers, and a sign-in lasts 7 days.

## Fixed

- **Removing a profile.** A profile removed just after it was closed could
  leave the list while its folder stayed on the disk. Removal now waits
  until the folder has gone, and says so if it will not go.
- **App packs in a check run.** A pack could be refused at start with a
  message about the rutba scheme, when it picked up a second copy of the
  core from a folder above the packs. Packs now only take packages from
  their own folder or the core, and a refused pack fails the checks.

## Not yet

- On Tall in Set up, dragging changes the tall layout, while the Place and
  size controls still act on the wide one.
- Wide and Tall in Set up are for Live Broadcast, not the Recorder.
- A web page's own sound plays to the computer's output and reaches a
  stream only through Computer sound.
- Scene names stay in the language in use when the setup was made.
- A recording into Video takes the sources shown when Record is pressed.
  Switching scene during the take does not change its layers.
