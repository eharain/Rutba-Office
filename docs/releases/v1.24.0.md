A picture from a file shows and can be deleted; a multilevel list in Rutba Word; find and replace across a presentation.

## Rutba Word

- **Home → Multilevel list, and the indent buttons on a list.** The
  multilevel list numbers 1., 1.1., 1.1.1., each level carrying the
  numbers above it; Increase and Decrease indent move a list paragraph a
  level rather than a step, so a numbered list nests 1. a. i. and a
  bulleted one changes its bullet, as Word's do. New list definitions are
  written with Word's nine levels, so there is somewhere to go; a
  paragraph in no list still moves its own indent. The button was greyed
  out.

- **Insert → Pictures draws the picture, and Delete takes it out.** A
  picture chosen from the file dialog was written as the digits of its
  byte array — the engine took a Buffer or base64 text and got neither —
  so it drew as nothing; it is written whole now, and .bmp and .webp are
  accepted along with png, jpeg and gif. A picked picture had no way out:
  Delete or Backspace now takes it, and its block when the block held
  nothing else, in one undo step.

## Presentation

- **Home → Find.** The words typed, Find lists every shape they are on
  across the deck with a count; a match clicked goes to its slide and
  selects the shape; Replace all rewrites them everywhere, run by run so
  each keeps its look, a field's text left alone, and says how many.
  Ctrl+F opens it. The Editing group was greyed out.

## Proven, not promised

- Two engine tests number five paragraphs, nest two of them to 2.1. and
  2.1.1., read the nine-level definition and the level back from the
  file; then nest a numbered list to a. and i., a bulleted one to its
  hollow bullet, and move a plain paragraph's indent. Three window checks
  press Multilevel list on two paragraphs and Increase indent on the
  second, find 1. and 1.1. on the page, then read the saved paragraph and
  definition, and put the paragraphs out of the list again (`look`).
- Two engine tests list every shape a word is on with counts, case
  aside or minded, then replace it across two slides and a footer, find a
  bold run and an italic one keeping their look, a slide-number field
  keeping its text, and the replacements in the saved file. Six window
  checks type a phrase, case-exact, find it with the title of slide 1
  first while on slide 2, click the match and land on the title, replace
  it across the deck with the count said and the lower-case words left
  alone, read the saved file, and put the words back (`find`).
- Two engine tests insert a picture as the byte array the dialog hands
  over and find the file's own bytes in the part and on the page, take it
  out with its block and undo; then accept bmp and webp, refuse tiff, and
  take a picture out of a paragraph of words leaving the words. Two window
  checks find the drawn picture decoded from the file's own bytes and
  delete it from the keyboard.
