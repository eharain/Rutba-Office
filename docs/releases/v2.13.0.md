Rutba Office 2.13.0 brings twenty-five sample businesses to Data Files, each a working database with a year of invented records or empty for your own, and print templates for what a business sends out: invoices, receipts, payslips and agreements, printed or saved as a PDF. Queries gain calculated columns written as Access writes them. Talk invites people by email, from your own address or from Rutba's, or by a link you send any way you like. Each app opens on New, its starter templates and the files you opened in it lately, and the templates now live in a repository of their own and say when they need a newer Office.

## Data Files: sample businesses

- **Twenty-five businesses in five groups.** The start screen offers a
  working database for each kind of small business Office is made for:
  shops and food (a corner store, a wine and deli shop, a cafe, a
  takeaway, a florist, a pharmacy counter); trade, making and building
  (a wholesaler, a workshop, a builder, a car dealer); services (a salon,
  a barber shop, a repair shop, a garage and MOT centre, a cleaning
  company, a consultancy, a gym); health, learning and community (a
  clinic, a training centre, a charity); and property, premises and staff
  (lettings, an estate agent, a guest house, a building's front desk, and
  HR, payroll and timesheets).
- **With sample records, or empty.** Choose a business and then With
  sample records, a year or more of invented trading made up a day at a
  time, to learn from; or Empty, the same tables, queries, forms, reports
  and print templates, ready for your own records.
- **Everything a business asks.** Each has its tables related, the
  questions that business asks as saved queries, a form for every table,
  reports, and print templates for what it sends out. Each carries a guide
  of its own, as full as Northwind's: what the business is, what each table
  holds, and what to try first.
- **On office.rutba.io too.** The same databases, each with its guide, are
  on office.rutba.io/samples to download.

## Data Files: print templates

- **Invoices, receipts, payslips and agreements.** A print template lays
  out one record as a business sends it: a heading, the record's fields,
  its lines (an invoice's items, a payslip's pay and deductions) with
  their totals, text with the record's values in it, and places to sign.
- **Your letterhead.** The business's name, address and details come from
  a table of its own, at the top of every template.
- **Paper to suit.** A4, Letter, A5, or an 80 mm or 58 mm till roll for a
  receipt.
- **Print or save as a PDF.** Print a template from the Print templates
  list, or from a form, for the record shown: Print and PDF sit in the
  form's bar. Design one in the window: its blocks, their fields, its
  paper.

## Data Files: queries

- **Calculated columns.** Write a column as Access writes one, Total:
  [Quantity]*[Price], with the functions people use: Round, Nz, IIf, Abs,
  Year, Month, Day, Weekday, Date, Now, DateDiff, Format, Len, Left, Right,
  Mid, UCase, LCase and Trim.
- **Formats as letters and invoices write them.** Numbers as 00042, 0.00
  or 1,234.50, dates as you choose, and the decimals each column shows.

## Talk: invite people

- **Asking emails them.** Adding someone by their address also tells them
  by email. Choose how: from your own address in Mail, sent from your
  account with a copy in Sent; or from Rutba, from no-reply@rutba.io in
  your name, a few a day and a week, the dialog saying how many are left;
  or no email, when they use Talk already. Each request you made says how
  its person was told.
- **A link for friends.** Invite with a link makes a link to send any way
  you like: copy it, or the message with it, send it with WhatsApp,
  Telegram, Facebook, X, LinkedIn, Mail or your own email app, or show it
  as a code a phone camera reads. Withdraw it when you are done.
- **Accepting.** The email or the link opens Talk on the invitation, who
  it is from and their note, with Accept. A friend without Rutba Office
  accepts on the link's page with a free Rutba account, and finds you
  there when they install Office and sign in. A link that did not open
  Office can be pasted into Open an invitation.
- **Your contacts as you type.** The address box suggests people from
  Contacts and Mail.

## Every app: where you start

- **New, starters and recent files.** Each app opens on New, its starter
  templates and the files you opened in it lately, with the time each was
  last opened. Remove one from the list and it stays removed.

## Templates

- **Their own repository.** The templates for Documents, Worksheets and
  Presentations, and the Data Files samples and ready-made databases, are
  kept in Rutba-Office-Templates, a public repository of their own, and
  come with Office as before.
- **They say what they need.** A template made for a newer Office says so
  in the gallery and on the start screen, and asks for Office to be
  updated, rather than opening wrong.
