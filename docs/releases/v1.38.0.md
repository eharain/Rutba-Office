Rutba Office in Urdu: every window's words, the frame right to left, and dates as Urdu writes them.

## Every app

- **Urdu**: the app menu's Language section offers English, اردو, or the
  system's own language. Every word the windows show goes through the
  message catalogue now, nearly 4,900 of them: the ribbons and their
  tooltips, the menus, the dialogs and panes, the status bars and the
  messages a window gives. The Urdu was drafted by machine against a
  glossary of the suite's recurring terms (`GLOSSARY-ur.md`, beside the
  catalogue) and awaits a native speaker's review.
- **Right to left**: a window in Urdu mirrors its frame. The ribbon reads
  from the right, the panes and dialogs follow, and the title bar's window
  buttons move to the left, as Windows places them for a right-to-left
  language. A document's page, a worksheet's grid and formula bar, and a
  slide's stage keep the direction their document gives them, so typing
  and editing work as they did.
- **Dates and numbers** in the window's language: the calendar's days and
  months, the dates in lists and details, and counts by Urdu's own plural
  rules.
- **A language is kept per window**, as Office keeps it: the windows opened
  after a choice open in it, the launcher at once, and every window after
  the next start.

## Changed in English

- A count of one now reads in the singular where it did not ("1 shape",
  "1 clip", "1 edit"), and a count of a thousand or more has its comma
  ("1,234 rows").

## Not yet in Urdu

- What the document engine and the services say in their own words (a
  refusal such as a wrong password, a file that will not open) and some
  names an engine hands the window (citation styles, envelope sizes, a
  query's steps) are still English, as is the installer. They come next.
