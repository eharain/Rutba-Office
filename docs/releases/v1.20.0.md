A picture goes into a worksheet.

## Worksheets

- **Insert → Pictures.** A picture from a file goes in at the cell, at its
  own proportions with its longer side no more than 320 px, drawn at once
  over the cells. The file carries it as Excel keeps a picture — a media
  part, the sheet's drawing part with a one-cell anchor at the cell and a
  relationship to the bytes — so Excel shows it too. One undo step, the
  parts going with it. The button was greyed out before; pictures in files
  were drawn and none could be added.

## Proven, not promised

- Two engine tests put a picture at a cell and read the media part, the
  anchor at the cell at its size, the relationship, the content type and
  the frame's drawing, from the file as well; a second picture, the undo
  that takes one away with its part, and a refused type.
- Three window checks put a picture at D6 through the op the button
  dispatches, read it drawn over the cell at its size from its own bytes,
  save, and read the media part and the anchor back from the file
  (`RUTBA_VERIFY_ONLY=sheetpic`).

## Under the hood

- `SheetView.insertPicture({ name, contentType, data, widthPx, heightPx })`
  over `ensureSheetDrawing`/`appendDrawingAnchor` and the shared
  `drawingAnchorXml` picture anchor; op `insertPicture`; the window reads
  the picture's pixels to size it.
