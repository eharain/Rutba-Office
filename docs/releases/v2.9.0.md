Rutba Office 2.9.0 brings Rutba Databases, the thirteenth app, for tables of records related to each other in one file. Presentations now formats the words you select rather than the whole box, Worksheets keeps styled words and .ods formatting through saving, AI Tools can answer AI apps on other machines when you choose, and the installer opens on the apps you use.

## Rutba Databases

- **Tables in one file.** A new database is one .rbase file on your
  computer. Make a table and it starts with an ID that numbers each
  record; add its fields in its design: short and long text, numbers,
  whole numbers, currency, dates and yes/no, each required, unique or
  with a default if you like.
- **Records.** A table's records open a page at a time, so a large one
  opens at once. Add a record from the last row, double-click a value to
  change it, sort by a column, and pick records to delete.
- **Relationships.** Relate a field in one table to a record in another,
  and the database keeps them from pointing at nothing. Choose whether
  deleting a record deletes the records that point at it.
- **SQL.** Ask a question in SQL and see the answer as a table. A query
  here only reads, so nothing in the database changes.
- **Import.** Bring in a CSV or TSV file, or an old file Worksheets reads
  (dBASE and FoxPro, Access, Lotus 1-2-3, SYLK, DIF), as tables of their
  own, with numbers, dates and yes/no read from their values.
- **Any SQLite database** opens too. Databases is one of the further apps:
  add it from Choose apps. Its name is still a working name.

## Presentations: format the words you select

- **Selected words alone.** Select a few words in a box and Bold, Italic,
  Underline, size, colour, font and highlight apply to those words only.
  With nothing selected they apply to the word at the caret, or to what you
  type next. Ctrl+B, Ctrl+I and Ctrl+U do the same.
- **Words as they look while you type.** The box shows its words in their
  own font, size and colour while you edit them, where they stand in the
  box.
- **Nothing lost.** Editing a line keeps every word's own look. Before, a
  bold or coloured word in a line lost its look once the line was edited.
- **Esc keeps your words**, as in PowerPoint. Insert Symbol puts its
  character at the caret in the box you are typing in.

## Worksheets keeps its formatting

- **Styled words in a cell.** A cell whose words have looks of their own,
  a bold word, a red phrase or a superscript, shows them. Edit the words
  and each part keeps its look; Bold and the other font buttons on the cell
  reach every part, as in Excel.
- **.ods files.** A workbook saved as .ods, or an .ods saved back, keeps
  its fonts, colours, fills, borders, alignment, merged cells, column
  widths and row heights, and opens again looking the same.

## AI Tools: this computer only, or other machines too

- **Your choice.** AI Tools has a Who can connect setting. This computer
  only, the default, is as before: nothing listens on the network.
- **Other machines too.** While the tools are on, Rutba Office also
  answers AI apps on other machines over the network, at the address AI
  Tools shows, to whoever has its key. The key stays hidden until you show
  it, and New Key shuts the old one out at once. Calls from web pages are
  refused.
- **Plainly said.** The connection is not encrypted, and AI Tools says so:
  use it on a network you trust, or through a secure tunnel. Each app on
  the network is listed with its address, and the log says where each call
  came from.

## Installer

- **It remembers.** The installer's Choose apps page opens with exactly
  the apps you have on, and the add-ons. Office keeps it up to date every
  time it starts.
