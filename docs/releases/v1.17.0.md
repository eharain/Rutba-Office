Frozen panes stay on screen, a picture never crosses a sheet's edge, and a note goes on a cell.

## Worksheets

- **Frozen panes stay on screen.** The frame has carried the frozen rows
  and columns in every viewport since panes could be frozen; the window
  drew them at their own place, so a frozen heading row scrolled away with
  the rest. Now the frozen rows sit under the column headings, the frozen
  columns beside the row headings, the corner at both, their headings with
  them — layers that stick, the way the headings already do. A press on a
  pinned cell selects it; the cell editor opens in whichever layer holds
  the cell.
- **A note goes on a cell.** Shift+F2, the Review tab's Note button or the
  cell's menu open a dialog for the words and the author (remembered for
  the next note; an unsigned one is signed with your account's name). On a
  noted cell the same dialog shows the note and offers Delete. A note is
  one undo step, and the file carries it as Excel keeps one: the comments
  part, the VML box Excel draws the words in, and the sheet pointing at
  both. A box Excel drew for another note is left as it was.

## Rutba Word

- **A picture never crosses a sheet's edge**, on screen or in print. A
  picture taller than the page's inside — a scanned card, portrait, at the
  column's width — was placed whole at the head of a page and the sheet's
  edge cut it; it is drawn to fit the inside now, its proportions kept. And
  a picture whose bytes arrived after the pass that placed its paragraph
  grew where the pass had left it, over the edge when the paragraph sat
  near the foot; its arrival now lays the pages out again.

## Proven, not promised

- Three engine tests hold a note's parts, relationships, content types and
  box, a replaced note, a removed one and the undo; one holds the print
  paginator to a picture that fits the page.
- Three window checks freeze at B2, scroll away and read the pinned row,
  column, corner and headings where they should be and nothing over them
  (`RUTBA_VERIFY_ONLY=freeze`); three put a note on through the dialog,
  save through the ribbon, read the file back and take the note off
  (`links`); two open a letter with a picture at a page's foot and one
  taller than a page and read each inside one sheet (`fit`).

## Under the hood

- `SheetPart.setLegacyDrawing`, `Workbook.comments/setComment/removeComment`,
  `parseComments`/`commentsXml`; `SheetView.setNote/removeNote`, the frame's
  `note`; ops `setNote`/`removeNote`.
- The grid's headings name both coordinates: an absolutely positioned
  heading with no `top` of its own took its static place, which the pinned
  wrapper in flow had moved down.
