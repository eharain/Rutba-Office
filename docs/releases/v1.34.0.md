Word's Multilevel List gallery, the rest of WordArt's warps, Home and End in Worksheets with a grid that follows the active cell, and a performance budget the windows are held to.

## Documents

- **Multilevel List gallery**: Home → Multilevel List opens Word's list
  library: 1. 1.1. 1.1.1., 1) a) i), I. A. 1., Article I. with Section
  1.01 and (a) under it, and the bullets. Each list is written into the
  document once, named, and numbered as Word numbers it, the Section level
  showing the Article's number in figures with a leading zero of its own.
  A document Word made with these lists opens numbered the same way.

## WordArt

- **The rest of the warps**: Transform → More now holds thirty, adding
  Square, the poured Arch: Up and Arch: Down, the cans, the rings, the
  cascades and Stop, in Documents, Worksheets and Presentations.

## Worksheets

- **Home, Ctrl+Home and Ctrl+End**: Home goes to the first column of the
  row, Ctrl+Home to A1 (or the first cell past frozen panes), Ctrl+End to
  the last cell in use, and Shift stretches the selection there, as
  Excel's do.

## Fixed

- Worksheets: an arrow, Enter, Tab or a jump that took the active cell out
  of sight left the grid where it was, with the selection off the screen;
  the grid now scrolls the least that shows the active cell.

## Checked

- **A performance budget in the windows**: a 300-page document, a
  100,000-row workbook and a 200-slide deck each open within twenty
  seconds and are taken to their end within eight, their window under
  1.5 GB. On this machine the document opened in about 2 seconds and
  scrolled to its end at once, the workbook opened in under 1 second and
  Ctrl+End reached its last row in a fifth of a second, and the deck opened
  in under 1 second, each window between 130 and 230 MB.
