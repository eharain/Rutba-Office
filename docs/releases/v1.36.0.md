Every one of Office's WordArt warps drawn, WordArt that prints as the page shows it, and Define New Multilevel List with lists linked to the heading styles.

## Documents

- **Define New Multilevel List**: Home → Multilevel List → Define New
  Multilevel List makes a list of one's own, a level at a time: its number
  style (1, 01, a, A, i, I, a bullet or none), the number's own words with
  the levels above it drawn in (Chapter %1., %1.%2), where it starts, where
  the number and the text sit, legal numbering, and a name. A preview
  numbers each level as it is set, by the same counting the page uses.
- **Lists linked to the heading styles**: a level can be linked to a
  paragraph style, so every Heading 1 reads Chapter I., II. and every
  Heading 2 1.1, 1.2 by its style, as Word links a heading to a list. A
  paragraph given the style later is numbered with it; a heading taken out
  of the list is taken out as Word does it. Files whose styles carry a list
  are now numbered when they open.
- **WordArt prints as it shows**: a text box under Transform is written to
  the PDF a letter at a time, along the arc, the circle or the button's
  lines, or stretched between a warp's two curves, laid out by the same
  code that draws it on the page. It printed straight before.

## Every app with WordArt

- **The last of the warp gallery**: Fade: Up and Fade: Down draw the words
  in towards the top or the foot; Deflate-Inflate and
  Deflate-Inflate-Deflate curve the line between their lines; and the
  poured Circle and Button are drawn round their Follow Path twins' paths.
  All thirty-six of Office's warps are now drawn, in Documents,
  Presentations and Worksheets.
