A floating picture prints where it sits, a worksheet's links and notes come alive, and Help opens a page that exists.

## Rutba Word: print and PDF wrap round a floating picture

A picture anchored at the left or the right has floated there on screen
since 1.11, the words wrapping round it. Print and PDF drew it as a block
under its paragraph. Now the paginator keeps the floats on each page with
the band they take and the side they take it on, lays the lines beside a
picture shorter — for as far down as it reaches, and into the paragraphs
after it — and places the picture at the paragraph's top on its side; the
PDF writer draws it there. A picture that will not fit goes whole onto the
next page with its paragraph. A centred or right-aligned picture drawn as a
block keeps its side. A floating text box still rides under its paragraph.

The gaps document had said the print paginator ignored a table's grid; it
has read the grid since the grid was parsed, and the document now says so.

## Worksheets: hyperlinks and notes

- **A link is read with its target** — an address, or a place in the
  workbook — drawn in the accent and underlined, with a tip that says where
  it goes. **Ctrl+click follows it**: an address opens outside the suite, a
  place (C12, Sheet2!B4, a name) is gone to, the other sheet first if it
  names one.
- **Ctrl+K, the Link button on Insert, or the cell's menu** puts a link on
  a cell: an address (a bare domain or an email is read as one) or a place,
  and a screen tip. On a linked cell the same dialog offers to change or
  remove it. A link is one undo step, and the file carries it as Excel
  writes one — an external relationship for an address.
- **A cell with a note wears Excel's red corner** and tells the note on
  hover, author first; the status bar counts the sheet's notes and says
  where the active cell's link goes. A workbook that carried notes used to
  show "[object Object]" as their tooltip. Writing a note is still to come.

## Mail

- **Get mail is F5**, the key every mail client fetches on. Ctrl+R was
  bound to both Get mail and Reply, and one key cannot do both.

## office.rutba.io

- **Help opens a page that exists.** Every Help tab in the suite has opened
  office.rutba.io/help since the tabs existed; the site had no such page.
  It now says how the suite works and what to press: opening, saving and
  recovery; the keys every app shares and the ones each app binds; each app
  with its formats; how a release arrives; where to say when something is
  wrong.

## Proven, not promised

- Three engine tests hold the hyperlinks block and its relationship
  through a save, a replace, a removal and an undo; one holds the floating
  picture's lines beside it and the picture in the PDF.
- Six application checks open a workbook made for the run with a note and
  a link, read the corner, the tip and the underline, follow the link to
  the other sheet, put a link on a cell through the dialog, save and read
  the file back through the engine, and take the link off. The address
  link is never clicked under the checks (`RUTBA_VERIFY_ONLY=links`).
- The viewer check's clips are now forty VP8 frames encoded with WebCodecs
  in one of the suite's own windows and muxed into a minimal WebM in the
  main process, the same on every machine; MediaRecorder captured nothing
  whenever another window was open, which failed the full run and passed
  alone. The Presentation fill check waits for the redraw it reads.

## Under the hood

- `wrapFirstLine` in `@rutba/drawing` breaks one line at a time so a
  paragraph's lines can differ in width; the paginator's `layoutAround`
  uses it, and each line carries its offset and width to the PDF writer.
- `SheetPart.hyperlinks/setHyperlink/removeHyperlink` and
  `Workbook.hyperlinks/setHyperlink/removeHyperlink`;
  `OoxmlPackage.addRelationshipTo` takes `{ external: true }`.
- The thumbnail queue no longer drops a job whose request was abandoned: a
  second request for the same file shared the job and inherited the
  abandonment.
