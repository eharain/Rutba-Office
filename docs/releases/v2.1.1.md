Rutba Office 2.1.1 fixes rotated objects. When a picture, shape or text box was turned, its selection frame and handles stayed upright, so clicks and drags missed the object; now the frame turns with it in Documents, Worksheets, Presentations and Image.

## Rotated objects

- **The frame turns with the object.** A turned shape, picture or text box
  shows its selection frame, its eight handles and its rotate handle turned
  with it about its centre, and the handles' cursors turn too. A click
  inside the turned outline takes hold of it; a click in the empty corner
  of its old upright box does not.
- **Resizing follows the object's own sides.** Dragging a handle of a
  turned object stretches it along its own sides, with the opposite handle
  staying where it is, as Word, Excel and PowerPoint do. Before, the object
  slid away from the handle and, after a few drags, could not be taken hold
  of at all.
- **More Rotation Options** sets the angle in degrees in Documents
  (Layout, Rotate), Worksheets (Page Layout, Rotate) and Presentations
  (Arrange, Rotate).
- **Documents**: a picture or shape in line with the text gets the rotate
  handle too, and the drawing follows its frame while a handle is dragged.
- **Worksheets**: on a sheet laid out right to left the frame stands on
  the turned drawing instead of turning the other way, and it is drawn
  over the drawing rather than under it. Charts still do not turn, as in
  Excel.
- **Presentations**: the text editor opens turned with its shape, a turned
  or flipped group or picture keeps its frame, the dashed frame round
  several shapes follows their turned outlines, and a click on the slide
  where no shape is lets go, as PowerPoint does.
- **Image**: a turned layer scales from its corner grip with the opposite
  corner staying put, where it drifted by as much as 85 pixels.
- The angle is kept in the saved file, and Undo and Redo restore it. Each
  app has a new window check (rotate-frame-word, rotate-frame-sheet,
  rotate-frame-deck, rotate-frame-image) at 30, 90 and 135 degrees.
