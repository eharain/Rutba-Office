Rutba Office 2.21.1 fixes two faults in 2.21.0. In Data Files, a table linked to a worksheet with a totals row showed its dates as numbers, and its sums carried long tails of digits. In Pictures, a drawing kept as an EMF or a WMF showed the picture icon on its tile instead of the drawing.

## Data Files: tables linked to a worksheet

- **Dates stay dates.** A column of dates with a row such as Total at its
  foot was read as text, so each date showed as the number Excel keeps it
  as: 46027 instead of 2026-01-05. Each date is now read as a date, and the
  Total row keeps its word.
- **Sums as the sheet shows them.** A cell that adds other cells, such as
  a week's takings, is read to the 15 figures Excel shows: 3588.39, not
  3588.3899999999994.

## Pictures

- **Drawings on their tiles.** Windows gives no small picture for an EMF
  or a WMF, so Pictures draws the tile itself, and it could not read the
  drawing it was given. The tile showed the picture icon. In the grid and
  in the strip along the foot of the window, an EMF's or a WMF's tile now
  shows its drawing, as an SVG's does.
