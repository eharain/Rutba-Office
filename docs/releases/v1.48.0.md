New ribbons in every app: one height on every tab, a picture for each command in the app's own colour, folding as the window narrows instead of scrolling, an icons-only mode, and KeyTips and arrow keys from the keyboard.

## Ribbons, in every app

- **Drawn to Office's measure, one height on every tab.** The page no
  longer jumps as you move between tabs. Tabs are names with the app's
  colour under the chosen one, and groups are three rows high, with large
  buttons showing clear pictures over names that wrap to two lines.
- **A picture for each command**, drawn in the app's own colour: 36 new
  ones where one picture used to stand for several commands, among them
  grow and shrink font, change case, clear formatting, the highlighter and
  font colour (each showing its colour), subscript and superscript, the
  paragraph mark, line spacing, borders and fill, every chart kind and each
  Function Library category in Worksheets. Mail's People has its own.
- **Menus look like menus.** A button that opens one carries a small
  arrow, and its menu drops from the button, wherever it was pressed.
  Worksheets' Paste options is the arrow half of a split Paste, as in
  Excel.
- **Documents' Styles** show each style as a sample on paper, as Word does.
- **A narrow window folds the ribbon instead of scrolling it.** From the
  end inwards, large buttons become small ones, then pictures alone, and
  last a whole group becomes one button that opens its controls under it
  (with a picture of its own for Styles, Themes and Variants). Nothing is
  cut off or pushed out of sight, and room is given back as the window
  widens.
- **Icons only**, from the ribbon's display options, for those who know
  the pictures: each name is then in its tooltip. It is remembered for
  every window.
- **From the keyboard**: Alt alone (or F10) shows KeyTips on the tabs,
  with Office's letters where Office has the tab, and a tab's letters
  show those of its controls; no code is the start of another. The arrow
  keys move along the tabs and into the controls with a ring that always
  shows, and Escape steps back and returns to the page.
- **Right to left**, in Urdu and Arabic, the ribbon's separators and
  arrows mirror, and its words are set a size up so they read.
- **Contextual tabs** (a table's, a picture's, a chart's) are marked in
  the app's colour.
- Some tooltips that used a long dash now read with a colon, in every
  language.

## Website pictures

- The pictures on office.rutba.io are taken by the suite itself. They now
  include PDF, opening a sample report, and Video, opening a project of
  sample stills, which had none.

## Checked

- A new window check, `ribbon` (63), opens all ten apps and photographs
  every tab at three widths, in the dark theme and in Urdu. It checks that
  the ribbon is one height on every tab, that nothing is cut off or left
  to scroll, that groups fold from the end and a folded group opens under
  its button and closes on Escape, that Icons only keeps every name in its
  tooltip, that KeyTips choose tabs with codes that never clash, and that
  the keyboard reaches the controls with a ring that shows and returns to
  the page.
- Every existing check that drives a ribbon ran against the new ones in
  the gate below. Five checks that the taller ribbon and a busier run
  exposed were put right, none of them a fault in the suite: the slideshow
  and clip-length check and the ribbon check now use folders of their own,
  the PDF recognition and table of contents checks wait for the page to
  draw what they count, and the kept templates are compared without the
  date in a notes master's date field, which made a run past midnight
  fail.

## Gate

Run on Windows (x64) on 2026-10-14, before 1.48.0 was released: 2,404
engine tests, 2,397 passing and 7 skipped where a package they need is not
installed; 12 of 12 editing checks; 1,586 of 1,587 application checks; and
11 of 11 windows painted clean.

The one application check that failed in that run (word: the table of
contents entries drawn on the page) read the page before it had drawn; it
now waits for it, and passed when run again on its own, as did the window
captures run after it.
