A Video editor with a timeline, quick edits and batch work in Image and Pictures, pages side by side in Documents, and zoom by Ctrl and the wheel.

## Every app that shows a page

- **Zoom with Ctrl and the wheel**: Ctrl and the mouse wheel zoom the page in
  Documents, the grid in Worksheets, the slide in Presentations and the
  message in Mail, 10% a notch, the way Word, Excel and PowerPoint do, and
  the point under the pointer stays where it was. Ctrl+Plus (Ctrl+= or
  Ctrl+Shift+=) and Ctrl+Minus step the zoom by 10%, and Ctrl+0 puts it back
  to 100%; the View menu's Zoom In, Zoom Out and Actual Size do the same. A
  touchpad pinch zooms smoothly. It is always the app's own zoom, the one the
  status bar's slider sets, so the ribbon and the panes keep their size.
- In Documents a page zoomed wider than the window now scrolls sideways;
  its right side was cut off before. In Pictures Ctrl+Plus now works, and
  Ctrl and the wheel no longer scroll the picture while zooming it.

## Documents

- **Pages side by side**: Documents lays its pages across the window once
  there is room, as Word does. At half size two or three pages stand side
  by side sharing their top edge, and at 30% six or more, the rows wrapping
  and centred. A right-to-left document lays them from the right. At 100%
  in a normal window the pages stand one under another as before. The zoom
  slider and Ctrl with the wheel lay the rows again as they change, and the
  slider now goes down to 30%.
- **View, Multiple Pages** chooses the zoom that shows two whole pages side
  by side, and One Page shows one.
- **View, Page Movement** offers Vertical and Side to Side. Side to Side
  puts every page in one row at a whole page's height, and the mouse wheel
  turns them sideways a page at a time, like a book. Vertical brings back
  the zoom it had.
- The ruler moves over the page the caret is on, and a press on any page,
  on its words or in its margin, puts the caret there. Typing at the foot
  of the left page carries on at the head of the right one.
- **Fixed**: a paragraph split at the foot of a page could move back and
  forth between the two pages after an edit until the layout gave up,
  leaving the pages after it overlapping. It now settles at once.

## Video

- **An editor on a timeline**: several clips, stills and sound laid end to
  end on lanes that show frames and waveforms. Split at the playhead (S, or
  Shift+S on every lane), trim with I and O or by dragging either end,
  ripple delete and ripple trim, Close Gap, and move or drag a clip between
  lanes with snapping to clips, markers and the beats of a song. Copy,
  paste, duplicate, Detach Audio and Link, markers, In and Out, undo and
  redo, frame steps and J, K and L. A right-click on a clip opens its own
  menu.
- **Per clip**: fit, fill or blur fill, quarter turns, flips and crop;
  volume, mute and fades; a track's mute, hide and lock.
- **Transitions and titles**: crossfade, dip to black, wipe, slide, push and
  zoom between clips. Titles, lower thirds, end cards and callouts on their
  own tracks, with fade, rise, type on and pop.
- **Export with sound** to MP4 or WebM, the whole project or only In to Out,
  in landscape, square or vertical frames.
- **Projects** are saved as `.rvid` files, opened again, and their media
  found again when it has moved. Unsaved work is kept for the launcher's
  Recover to bring back. The timeline runs left to right in every language,
  and the editor's words are in Urdu, Arabic and Hindi too.

## Image

- **Quick edits**: flip vertical, resize by pixels or percent, invert, and
  straighten by an angle keeping the largest upright rectangle. Pen,
  rectangle, ellipse, arrow and text marks drawn on the picture stay
  editable until Done.
- **Tone**: auto enhance, levels, curves, white balance, exposure,
  highlights and shadows, plus sharpen, noise reduction, vignette and red
  eye, all worked on the pixels away from the window so it keeps
  answering.
- **Clipboard and export**: copy the picture, paste one as a new picture,
  and export with a JPEG or WebP quality, a size cap, a longest side, and
  the metadata stripped or kept.

## Pictures

- **Batch edit**: the pictures in a folder resized (by a percentage, to fit
  inside a box, or by the longest side), converted to PNG, JPEG or WebP,
  renamed by a pattern of name, number, date and size, turned upright by
  their EXIF orientation, with metadata stripped or kept. They are written
  beside the originals or in a chosen folder, and a file already there is
  never replaced without asking.

  presses on the right-hand page, types across the fold, and tries
  Multiple Pages and Side to Side. `zoom` zooms the four apps by keys,
  wheel and pinch. `image-edits` goes through every quick edit, tone tool,
  mark, export and a batch run. `video-editor` builds two clips with a tone
  each and a still at run time, edits them, exports, and reads the file
  back for its length and tracks.
