Rutba Office 2.3.0 lets you work on Video's preview itself. Anything that shows there can be picked, moved, resized and turned to any angle. Add Media asks where files go. The In and Out points and markers drag along the timeline. Tracks split, insert, move, solo and lock. Every window now says it is Rutba Office. A link in an email opens in your browser, and junk's links ask first.

## Every window says it is Rutba Office

- Every app's title bar begins with the Rutba Office mark and name, then
  the app's own. In a narrow window only the mark stays. The launcher
  shows the mark itself.

## Mail: links

- **A link opens in your browser.** Before, a click on a link in a
  message turned the reading pane blank. Now the link opens in your
  browser and the message stays where it was. A mailto: link starts a new
  message to that address. Any other kind of link is not opened, and Mail
  says so.
- **Links that might be traps ask first.** Mail warns before opening a
  link in any of these cases:
  - the message is in Junk;
  - the message failed its sender's checks (SPF, DKIM, DMARC);
  - the link's words name a different site from the one it goes to;
  - the address is a bare number, a lookalike name, or hides its real
    site behind an @.
- **The warning** shows the address in full, with Open in Browser, Copy
  Link and Cancel. Cancel is the default.
- The message still runs no scripts. Only a click can follow a link, and
  nothing loads into the reading pane.

## Speed

- **Save after Encrypt with Password.** Save no longer holds every window
  for a fifth of a second when it comes just after Encrypt with Password.
  The password's key is worked out on its own thread first, which Save,
  Export and the recovery copy now wait for.

## Video: the preview

- **Pick anything you can see.** A click on the preview picks the clip,
  still, title or shape under the pointer, the top one first, and picks
  it on the timeline too. The picked one wears a frame turned with it.
- **Move, resize and turn it there.** A drag inside moves it. A corner
  grip resizes it from the opposite corner, which stays where it stands
  even when the clip is turned. The round grip above turns it to any
  angle, and Shift turns it in steps of 15 degrees.
- **Picture in picture for any clip or still.** A clip or still that fills
  the frame has corner grips only, and the first drag of one makes it a
  picture in picture. The inspector's Picture in picture button does the
  same, and Fill the whole frame puts a still back.
- **Position and size in numbers.** The inspector has across, down, width,
  height and angle for every picture, plus opacity for titles as well
  as pictures, and a blend mode (Multiply, Screen, Overlay, Darken,
  Lighten, Difference). The export draws what the preview shows, because
  both are drawn by the same painter.
- A clip on a locked track is not picked on the preview.

## Video: the timeline

- **Add Media asks where.** Add Media puts files after the clips on the
  picked track (V1 when nothing is picked). Add on a New Track puts them
  on a track of their own above the others, from the playhead. Insert at
  Playhead splits the clip under the playhead and moves what follows
  later to make room, its sound with it. Several sound files now go one
  after another on one track; each used to get a track of its own.
- **In, Out and markers that drag.** The In and Out points are flags on
  the ruler that drag along it, snapping to clip edges and the playhead
  (Alt frees them). They also show on the preview's scrub bar, where they
  drag too. A marker drags along the ruler; a click without a drag still
  takes the playhead to it.
- **Tracks.** Edit, Tracks has these commands:
  - Split Track: the clips on the picked track from the playhead on move
    up onto a new track of their own, so clips can sit next to each other
    on two tracks.
  - Insert Track and Move Track Up and Down: each track keeps its state as
    it moves.
- **Solo.** Each track has a solo button. While a track is soloed, the
  other tracks of its kind are left out of the preview and the export.
- **Locks hold.** A locked track's clips are kept by every edit (split,
  delete, trim, the inspector), with a message that says why. Before,
  locking only stopped dragging.

## Presentations and accessibility

- **Slide strip and Design gallery.** Each slide in the strip and each
  theme and variant in the Design gallery is now a picture with its button
  beside it, not inside it. The words drawn in a picture are no longer
  taken for the button's label, so a screen reader or voice control hears
  the button's name. Clicks, hover tips and the keyboard ring are as
  before.
- **axe-core 4.14.0.** The accessibility audit moves to 4.14.0, which
  checks this rule (WCAG 2.5.3, label in name). Every window passes it.
