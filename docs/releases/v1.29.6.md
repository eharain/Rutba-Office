Tables in Documents that do what Word's do (Table Design, Table Layout, formulas, Draw Table and the Eraser), right to left in the ruler, sheets and slides, OpenDocument, Rich Text and web pages opened and saved whole, and password-protected Office 97 to 2003 files opened with their password.

## Documents

- **A Table Design tab**: Table Styles puts a table in Table Grid, Plain
  Table 1, Grid Table 1 Light, or Grid Table 4 or List Table 4 in black or
  any of the six accents, written in the document's theme colours as Word
  writes them; Table Style Options turn the header row, total row, banded
  rows and columns and the first and last column on and off; Shading
  colours the selected cells; Borders draws with a pen in Word's line
  styles, weights and colours.
- **A Table Layout tab**: rows and columns in and out, merge and split
  cells (a cell into columns and rows, or a merged cell back into its own),
  Split Table, column width, AutoFit and Distribute Columns, Align Table,
  the words at the top, centre or bottom of a cell, turned to read down or
  up, the cells' margins, Repeat Header Rows, Sort, Convert to Text (and
  Insert's Convert Text to Table), right to left, View Gridlines and Table
  Properties: the table's width, alignment and alt text, the row's height
  and whether it may break across pages.
- **Draw Table and the Eraser**: the pen draws a new table on the page, or
  a line down or across a cell to split it there; the eraser joins the two
  cells either side of the line it is clicked on.
- **Formulas in tables**: `=SUM(ABOVE)`, `=AVERAGE(LEFT)` and the like,
  cells as Word names them (`=B2*C2`, `=SUM(B2:B4)`), arithmetic and
  brackets, in a number format; Update all formulas works them out again.
- **Tables are drawn as Word draws them**, on screen, on paper and in a
  PDF: in their own lines and shading, in their table style's header row,
  bands and columns, a merged cell as one cell over the rows it covers,
  in their own cell margins and where their alignment puts them, and the
  header rows again at the head of every page the table runs onto.
- **Check Accessibility** reads a table cell's words against the cell's own
  shading and its table style's, and offers to unmerge a table's merged
  cells in one click.
- **Right to left reaches the ruler, tab stops, tables and sections**: in a
  right-to-left paragraph the ruler and its tab stops count from the right
  margin, a table can run from the right (Table Layout → Right to Left),
  and a section lays its columns from the right.

## Worksheets

- **Page Layout → Sheet Right-to-Left** puts column A at the right, as an
  Arabic or Hebrew workbook reads.
- **SUBTOTAL leaves out hidden rows**, as Excel's does: 1 to 11 the rows a
  filter hides, 101 to 111 every hidden row.
- **Data → Auto Outline** groups the rows each total adds up above it and
  the columns each row total adds up to its left, nested as deep as the
  totals go.
- **Advanced Filter copies a list from another sheet** to the one it is
  started on, the list named with its sheet (`Data!A1:D20`), as in Excel.

## Presentations

- **A Table Design tab**: Table Style Options and Shading for the selected
  table.
- **A paragraph reads right to left**: drawn from the box's right edge with
  its indent and bullet on that side, and Home → Paragraph turns one either
  way.

## Files

- **OpenDocument text opens and saves whole**: headings, runs in their
  looks, links, lists at their levels, tables with merged cells, pictures,
  notes, headers and footers.
- **Rich Text and web pages open as documents**, and a document saved as
  Rich Text, HTML or plain text keeps its headings, list numbers, tables and
  pictures (Rich Text lists as real lists Word continues).
- **OpenDocument spreadsheets and presentations keep their charts, shapes
  and pictures**, in and out; a stacked chart stays stacked, and an ODF
  custom shape is drawn as its own outline.
- **A password-protected Word, Excel or PowerPoint 97 to 2003 file opens with
  its password**: RC4 CryptoAPI as Office 2003 onwards writes it, Office
  97/2000's RC4 and Excel 5.0/95's obfuscation.

## Mail

- **Junk options per account**, as Outlook keeps them; mail to a group or a
  mailing list you belong to is never junked; mail from chosen countries'
  domains or in chosen encodings can be blocked.

## Behind the scenes

- The windows' words go through a message catalogue, counted by each
  language's own plural rules: the groundwork for translations; no
  language but English yet.
- The release workflow runs on actions/checkout and actions/setup-node v5.

## Fixed

- Documents: an .odt opened as plain paragraphs, and saving as .odt wrote
  one; its headings, lists, tables and pictures were lost.
- Documents: saving as Rich Text, HTML or plain text wrote the editor's
  plain lines, without headings, list numbers, tables or pictures.
- Documents: a numbered list saved to Markdown lost its numbers, and a
  nested list its levels.
- Documents: Insert Table's "repeat the first row as a header" box did
  nothing.
- Documents: Check Accessibility measured a table cell's words against the
  page, not the cell's shading.
- Worksheets and Presentations: an OpenDocument chart lost its stacking,
  and an ODF custom shape came in as a box.
