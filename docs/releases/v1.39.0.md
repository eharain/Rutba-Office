The rest of Rutba Office in Urdu: what the engines and services say for themselves, and the names they hand the windows.

## Every app

- **Messages from the engines in Urdu**: a refusal or a failure the
  document thread, a file reader or a mail, calendar or contacts service
  gives in its own words (a wrong password, a file that will not open, a
  step a document cannot take) now reaches an Urdu window in Urdu, some
  310 of them. The engines keep writing them in English, for the logs and
  for anyone reading a report, and the window finds the translation as the
  message arrives, putting back the file names, style names and numbers it
  carried.
- **Open files with Rutba Office** gives its instructions and its result in
  the window's language.

## Documents

- The **citation styles' editions**, the **source types** and every
  **bibliography field** in Create Source, the **Table of Authorities
  categories** in Mark Citation and Insert Table of Authorities, a
  **field's description and text formats** in Insert Field, the **envelope
  sizes**, and the **label kinds** and each label's line in Labels and
  Label Options show in Urdu.

## Worksheets

- The Power Query editor's **Applied steps** read in Urdu, each step in a
  sentence of its own so the column, the value and the verb fall in Urdu's
  order, and Merge Queries' **join kinds** with them.

## Checked

- A test holds every engine message to its own Urdu: each is filled the
  way an engine throws it and must come back as its translation, not as a
  looser message it also happens to fit.

## Not yet

- The installer is still English, and the whole of the Urdu, the 1.38.0
  catalogue and these, awaits a native speaker's review.
