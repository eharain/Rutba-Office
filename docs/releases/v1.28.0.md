An index and a table of figures, and page numbers, dates and the file's name as fields anywhere in the text, in Rutba Word; a workbook dressed in a theme, its colours, fonts and effects, in Worksheets.

## Rutba Word

- **References → Mark Entry (Alt+Shift+X).** A Mark Index Entry box that
  stays beside the page while you work. It takes the selected words as the
  main entry, with a subentry, a "See" cross-reference, a page range from a
  bookmark, and bold or italic page numbers; Mark All marks the words each
  time they begin a paragraph's mention of them. Each mark is Word's hidden
  XE field, shown when ¶ is on, and the paragraph stays editable around it.
- **References → Insert Index.** A dialog that previews the index before it
  goes in — indented or run-in, columns, page numbers right-aligned behind a
  leader — and writes Word's INDEX field, grouped by letter, with the pages
  each entry lies on as this window lays them out. Update Index and F9 bring
  it up to date with the marks as they now stand. The column count is kept in
  the field for Word; this window and its printout lay the index in one
  column.
- **References → Insert Table of Figures.** The captions of one label —
  Figure, Table, Equation — each linked to its caption with its page, with or
  without the label and number, page numbers right-aligned behind a leader.
  Update Table, beside Cross-reference in the Captions group as in Word,
  rebuilds it, and F9 does too. A table of contents and a table of figures no
  longer take each other's bookmarks away when either is rebuilt, and Insert
  Caption lands after the caret's paragraph when a table of contents sits
  above it.
- **Insert → Quick Parts → Field.** A page number, the page count, the date
  or the time, the file's name, the author or the title, put into the text
  in a format of your choosing — Word's date and number formats and its case
  options — as the fields Word writes, with the result and the field code
  shown before OK. F9 works them out again from the pages as they now fall,
  and so does printing. Quick Parts also puts the author or the title in with
  one press.

## Worksheets

- **Page Layout → Themes, Colours, Fonts and Effects.** A workbook dressed in
  one of the suite's eleven themes — the same ones Presentation offers — each
  gallery drawing a small sheet in every choice. Customise Colours and
  Customise Fonts save a theme of your own. Cells filled in theme colours,
  tables, charts, shapes and the default font follow the theme, the theme is
  written into the workbook as Excel writes one, and Undo puts the old one
  back. A chart or a shape keeps the suite's own colours while the workbook
  is on an Office theme, as before; on the dark themes the sheet keeps white
  paper.

## Proven, not promised

- Six engine tests in `tests/word-index.test.js`: the XE and INDEX field
  codes as Word writes them; entries sorted under their letters, each page
  once, ranges and cross-references; a mark that survives typing and
  deleting beside it; Mark All; an INDEX field that follows new marks and
  keeps its page numbers when no fresh ones are given; run-in with a bookmark
  range. The window check `wordindex` marks, indexes, updates and saves
  through the ribbon and Alt+Shift+X.
- Five in `tests/word-figures.test.js`: a `TOC \c` field with a bookmark and
  a PAGEREF for each caption; Update Table following a new caption while the
  table of contents keeps its own bookmarks; no page numbers and no right
  alignment kept through an update; Word's date and number formats; body
  fields that stay editable and that F9 works out again. The window check
  `figures` puts PAGE, NUMPAGES and DATE in from the Field dialog, lists and
  updates a table of figures, and watches F9 carry "Page 2 of 2" to "Page 3
  of 3".
- Four in `tests/sheet-themes.test.js` hold the workbook's `theme1.xml`, the
  theme colours of cells, tables, charts and shapes, and undo. The window
  check `sheetthemes` applies a theme, a colour set and a font set from the
  galleries and saves them, beside the deck's own `themes` check, which still
  passes on the theme data the two apps now share.
