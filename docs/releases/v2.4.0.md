Rutba Office 2.4.0 adds AI Tools. An AI app on this computer can now make and read documents, workbooks, presentations and PDFs with Rutba Office, in folders you choose. The tools are off until you turn them on, for this session or always, and nothing listens on the network.

## AI Tools

- **Off unless you turn them on.** In any app, open the app menu and choose
  AI Tools. The setting is Off, On for this session (until Rutba Office
  closes) or Always on. It is read again for every call, so turning the
  tools off stops the next call.
- **In folders you choose.** Turning the tools on asks for a folder first.
  If you cancel, they stay off, and no folder is made for you. You can add
  more folders or remove any. A path outside them is refused, even one that
  leads out through a link.
- **Only this computer.** The AI app starts the tools itself and talks to
  them through standard input and output (the Model Context Protocol,
  MCP). Nothing listens on the network, so no other machine can connect. A
  test fails the release if the tools ever bring in a networking module.
- **What they do:**
  - list the folders and the files in them;
  - read a document, a workbook, a presentation, a PDF or a text file;
  - make a document (in a theme, with headings, lists and tables), a
    workbook (formulas too) or a presentation;
  - start from a template;
  - make a PDF of a document;
  - make a new PDF from pages of others;
  - read the words in a PNG picture, on this computer.
- **Nothing is overwritten or deleted.** A file the tools make must have a
  name of its own.
- **See who is connected.** While the tools are on, every window's title bar
  shows a badge, with a count when an AI app is connected. Click it to see
  each app's name and version, that it is on this computer, when it
  connected and its last call. AI Tools also lists what the tools have done.
- **Connect an app.** AI Tools shows the lines to add to an AI app's MCP
  servers, with a Copy button. docs/AI-TOOLS.md has the details.
