Worksheets select, copy and fill the way Excel does: a range chosen with the mouse, copy, cut and paste through the system clipboard, Paste Special with its operations and Transpose, and the fill handle's series and Auto Fill Options.

## Worksheets

- **A range is chosen with the mouse**: a drag from one cell to another
  selects the cells between, and held past the edge of the grid it scrolls
  on and keeps selecting; a drag across the column or row headings selects
  whole columns or rows; Ctrl starts another range beside the one there.
- **Copy, Cut and Paste work through the system clipboard**: Ctrl+C puts
  the cells there as words for any application and as a table a
  spreadsheet reads by cell; Ctrl+V pastes them, formulas moving with them
  and their formatting with them; Ctrl+X and Ctrl+V move the cells, their
  formulas as they were, their old place left empty. Cut, Copy, Paste and
  Paste Special are on the right-click menu too.
- **Paste Special** (Ctrl+Alt+V, or the arrow beside Paste): all of the
  copied cells, their formulas, their values or their formatting alone; an
  operation — add, subtract, multiply or divide — that works the copy out
  against the cells already there, a number against a number, joined to a
  formula, words left as they are; Skip blanks; and Transpose, rows to
  columns, its formulas turned with it. The arrow beside Paste offers
  Formulas, Values, Formatting and Transpose in one click.
- **The fill handle continues a series**: two numbers dragged down carry
  on by their step, a date by days, a month name by months, as before —
  and now from the whole selection, where it filled from the active cell
  alone. Hold Ctrl to copy a series, or make a series of a single number.
  **Auto Fill Options**, at the corner of the cells just filled, turns the
  fill into Copy Cells, Fill Series, Fill Formatting Only or Fill Without
  Formatting.
- **Home → Fill → Series**: down the columns or along the rows, linear,
  growth, or dates by day, weekday, month or year, with a step and a stop;
  Fill Up and Fill Left beside Down and Right.

## Fixed

- Worksheets: Ctrl+C put nothing on the system clipboard and Ctrl+V did
  nothing; only the ribbon's Paste pasted, as plain words.
- Worksheets: the fill handle sat at the active cell's corner and filled
  from that cell alone, so a selected run of numbers could not be carried
  on; Ctrl+D and Ctrl+R over a range, Row Height and Column Width, AutoFit
  and Add Watch likewise took the active cell for the whole selection.
