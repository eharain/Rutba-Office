Motion paths and effects by paragraph in Presentations, 3D models in Presentations and Documents drawn on this computer, Cameo with the camera live on a slide, and the mail merge rules Ask, Fill-in and Set Bookmark.

## Presentations

- **Motion Paths**: Animations → Motion Paths, and a Motion Paths heading
  in More Effects and Add Animation, move a shape from where it stands
  down, up, left or right, along an arc or a turn, round a circle or a
  square, or through a loop. Each is written as PowerPoint writes a path
  drawn by hand, so PowerPoint plays it as its own. The path of the picked
  shape is drawn on the stage from its centre, green where it starts and
  red where it ends. In the show the shape travels along the path and stays
  where it ends; a second path starts there, and a step back puts it where
  the show has it.
- **Effect Options → Sequence**: a text shape's entrance, emphasis or exit
  plays as one object, all at once, or by paragraph, each paragraph on a
  click of its own. The Animation Pane lists each paragraph's effect by its
  words, and PowerPoint reads them as its own paragraph effects.
- **3D Models**: Insert → 3D Models reads a glTF model (a .glb, or a
  .gltf with its files beside it) and puts it on the slide as the picture
  the suite's own renderer draws of it: lit, textured, smooth at its edges,
  the same on every computer. The 3D Model tab turns it to Front, Back,
  Left, Right, Top, Bottom or one of four corners above and below, turns it
  fifteen degrees at a time, and resets it; a handle in its middle turns it
  as it is dragged, drawn small while the pointer moves. The model is kept
  beside its picture in the file, so PowerPoint and every other reader show
  the picture.
- **Cameo**: Insert → Cameo and Record → Cameo put a cameo at the slide's
  lower right. The show fills it with the computer's camera, live, mirrored
  as a camera pointed at oneself is and cropped to its shape; Camera Format
  → Preview shows it on the stage, and Camera Styles makes it a rectangle,
  an oval or a rounded rectangle. The camera is opened only while a cameo is
  shown and closed with the last of them. Any other reader shows a grey
  shape with a camera drawn in it.

## Documents

- **3D Models**: Insert → 3D Models puts a glTF model at the caret as the
  picture the suite draws of it, in a paragraph of its own; the 3D Model tab
  turns it to any of the ten views, fifteen degrees at a time and back to
  where it went in. One Undo brings back the old picture with the old view.
  Word opens the document with the picture in place.
- **Mail merge Rules → Ask, Fill-in and Set Bookmark**, each with Word's
  own dialog and the field Word writes. An Ask or a Set can show its
  bookmark's value straight after it. Finish & Merge asks the merge's
  questions before it runs: one answer for a question asked once, one for
  each record otherwise, each filled with its default; every letter,
  message or printed copy takes its record's answers.
