WordArt's Transform in Documents, Worksheets and Presentations, the last open findings of the 1.29 reading put right but two, and a network share no longer looked at because a file named it.

## WordArt

- **Transform**: Shape Format → Text Effects → Transform lays a WordArt's
  or a text box's words along Arch Up, Arch Down, Circle or Button, in
  Documents, Worksheets and Presentations. The words are drawn along the
  arc, round the circle or over the button on the page, the sheet and the
  slide, in a slide's thumbnail and in the show, each line at the size that
  fills its part of the path inside the box. They are saved as Word, Excel
  and PowerPoint save them, so Office shows the same arch, and a file Office
  made with a Transform opens with it. No Transform puts the words straight
  again. In Documents the words show straight while the caret is in them,
  to be edited, and go back along the path when it leaves.

## Documents

- **Merging or splitting cells with Track Changes on asks first**, as Word
  does: neither is recorded as a change, here or in Word, and Word says so
  before it does either. Asked from the ribbon and from a table's
  right-click menu alike, and Cancel leaves the table as it was.

## Fixed

- Worksheets: the fill handle copied a merged block's values and look but
  not its merge; each merge is repeated where it falls in the new cells, as
  Excel's fill repeats it, and Fill Without Formatting takes none.
- Worksheets: a Power Query column given a type lost it through append,
  merge, group, rename and the other steps, and a column made dates loaded
  as bare day numbers; a type follows its column, and a date column loads
  in the short date format.
- Documents: the split view's lower pane copied the whole document a moment
  after every change, every page of a long one; it copies only the
  paragraphs typed in.
- Presentations: a screen recording was gathered whole in the window and
  sent to the deck in one piece, about 19 MB a minute; it goes by a scratch
  file a few megabytes at a time, and the file is removed once the slide
  has it, or on Cancel.
- Contacts: every card's photo went to the window on every list and every
  search; each photo is fetched when its card is drawn, and kept until the
  card changes.
- Screenshot: taking a window drew every window and every screen at full
  size to keep one; only the kind asked for is drawn.
- Pictures, Video and Image: a picture or a clip on a network share was
  looked at whenever a window asked, and looking at a share hands it this
  computer's Windows sign-in; one is shown now only once you reached the
  share yourself in this run, by opening a file there, browsing its folder
  or picking it in a dialog. A file on this computer is shown as before.
