Documents, workbooks and decks are worked on off the main process, so every other window answers while one is busy, and a slide show keeps every quick press.

## Every document app

- **Documents are worked on off the main process**: opening, editing,
  laying out, printing and saving every document, workbook and deck now
  happen on a thread of their own. The main process only passes the
  windows' requests in and the answers out, so mail, the launcher, the menu
  and the other windows answer at once while a long document is laid out
  for print or a large workbook recalculates. Equations are still laid out
  by Chromium, and pictures still reach the window that asked for them.
- **If that thread stops**, it is started again: the documents it held are
  closed, their windows are told so in a sentence, and the unsaved work in
  each, up to its last half-minute copy, is offered back by the launcher.
  On a machine where the thread cannot start at all, the documents are
  worked on by the main process as before.

## Presentations

- A click or a key pressed in a show just as the next slide arrived was
  lost, so a show set to loop could stop at the end of its slides, and a
  click after an action had jumped to a slide did not move the show on.
  The press is kept and carried out when the slide is there.

## Calendar and Contacts

- An event or a card made while a sync was sending to a CalDAV or
  CardDAV server was never sent: the sync cleared the whole queue when it
  finished. It stays queued for the next sync. A Sync pressed while one
  runs now waits for it and runs once more, so a change made a moment
  before goes up with it.
