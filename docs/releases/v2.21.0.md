Rutba Office 2.21.0 opens many more kinds of picture. Pictures and Image read TIFF a page at a time, Photoshop documents, EMF and WMF drawings, and a run of smaller formats, and on Windows they show HEIC, JPEG XR and camera RAW through the codecs Windows already has. A chart or clip art kept as an EMF or a WMF is now drawn in documents, workbooks and presentations. Remove location takes the place out of a picture and keeps the camera's details. The installer is smaller, a slide show no longer flickers, and the message you are writing in Mail is laid out properly wherever it is open.

## More pictures

- **TIFF, a page at a time.** A TIFF opens in Pictures, Image, Documents,
  Worksheets and Presentations, compressed or not. A fax or a scan of
  several pages shows its pages in a strip, and Image opens the page you
  choose.
- **Photoshop documents** (.psd and .psb) open in Pictures and Image as the
  one picture Photoshop shows.
- **EMF and WMF drawings** open in Pictures, drawn as lines, shapes and words
  rather than as a flat picture.
- **Smaller formats:** QOI, Netpbm (PBM, PGM and PPM), Targa, PCX, Radiance
  HDR and cursors open in Pictures and Image.
- **HEIC, HEIF, JPEG XR and camera RAW** (DNG, CR2, NEF, ARW, ORF, RW2 and
  RAF) show in Pictures at the size of your screen, read by Windows' own
  codecs. HEIC needs the HEIF Image Extensions and HEVC Video Extensions
  from the Microsoft Store, and RAW needs the Raw Image Extension. A RAW
  picture with no codec shows the full-size JPEG its camera put inside it,
  and where there is nothing to show Pictures says which extension it
  needs.
- **Image saves more.** BMP, ICO and TIFF sit beside PNG, JPEG and WebP, and
  Image opens an SVG at the size you choose.
- A damaged picture is refused in a sentence, never a hang.

## Drawings in documents

- **Charts and clip art kept as EMF or WMF** are drawn in Documents,
  Worksheets and Presentations where Office draws them, their lines,
  shapes, words and pictures in place. Before, a drawn EMF showed only its
  frame, and Documents and Worksheets drew none.
- A TIFF or Targa picture inside a document is drawn too.

## Picture details

- **Remove location.** On Image's export and in Pictures' Batch edit, Remove
  location takes out where a picture was taken and keeps the camera's
  details, beside Strip all.
- **Details kept on export.** JPEG, PNG, WebP and TIFF exports keep the
  original's details, from a picture with layers too.
- **Details from more pictures.** The camera's details are read from PNG,
  WebP, HEIC and AVIF beside JPEG and TIFF.
- **Windows' own stars and tags.** The rating and keywords that Windows
  Photos, Explorer and Lightroom write into a picture show in Details
  beside Pictures' own, and Use takes them as Pictures' own.

## Fixes

- **Slide shows.** A slide show stays full screen from slide to slide, and
  the taskbar no longer flickers as each slide comes. Reading View stays in
  its window.
- **Writing a message.** The message being written is laid out the same in
  the reading pane, in full view and in a window of its own: To, Cc and
  Bcc are as wide as Subject, with their names beside them, and the
  message fills the space below. In a narrow reading pane the buttons wrap
  rather than run off the edge.
- **A smaller installer.** The apps inside the installer are compressed
  harder, so it drops from 176 MB to 149 MB.
- **AI Tools removed and added back.** Removing AI Tools while an AI app was
  connected left part of it behind, and adding it back then failed. AI
  Tools is now left whole while an app holds it, and Add takes the removal
  back.
- **AI Tools in an AI app installed from the Store.** An AI app installed
  as a Windows app package could read an old copy of Office's settings and
  refuse every request. With Rutba Office running, the tools now take its
  settings from the running copy, and their log and connections show in
  the AI Tools window.

## Languages

- The new messages are in Urdu, Arabic and Hindi.

## Not in this release

- A Photoshop document's layers open in Image in a later version.
- HEIC and camera RAW open in Pictures only, not yet in Image.
- Gmail and Outlook sign-in, and Google Sheets as a source, wait for the
  sign-in registrations.
- A drawing's text written by glyph number, as some programs write it, is
  left out.
