Seventeen more buttons work (Immersive Reader, Screenshot, Icons, Photo Album, Action, Trigger, Rehearse Timings and Design's themes in Documents among them), and a heading Word colours from its theme now takes the theme's colours here too.

## Documents

- **View → Immersive Reader.** A tab of its own that turns the pages into a
  column for reading: Column Width, Page Color in sepia or inverse, Line
  Focus on one, three or five lines that the arrow keys move the words
  through, Text Spacing, Syllables shown as dots in long words, and Read
  Aloud. Close puts the pages back; nothing is written to the file.
- **Design → Themes, Colours, Fonts and Effects.** The document's theme
  part, rewritten as Word writes it, one undo step each. A colour named by
  theme slot (Word's headings are accent 1, darkened) is now drawn from
  the theme rather than from the hex written beside it, so headings take
  the new colours, and the styles take the new heading and body faces.
- **Design → Set as Default.** After asking, new blank documents start in
  this document's theme and styles, as Word's Normal template makes them.
- **Insert → Screenshot** and **Insert → Icons** (below).

## Presentations

- **Insert → Action.** What a click on a shape does in the show: the next,
  previous, first or last slide, the end of the show, a slide of the deck
  or a web address. Kept on the shape as PowerPoint keeps it, and obeyed
  in the show, in a kiosk too. A slide is kept as a relationship, so the
  action follows it when slides move.
- **Insert → Photo Album.** A new presentation of your pictures in a window
  of its own: a title slide, then the pictures one, two or four to a slide
  in the order you set, each fitted without stretching, captioned with its
  name when asked.
- **Animations → Trigger.** An effect started by a click on a shape in the
  show (a quiz's answer, a hidden note), kept as PowerPoint keeps it, in a
  sequence of its own. The show keeps what it brings in hidden until the
  click, plays it then without moving on, and a click anywhere else still
  moves the show on. A trigger PowerPoint wrote is now read and played too.
- **Slide Show → Rehearse Timings.** The show from its start with a clock in
  the corner; at the end it asks whether to keep each slide's time, and
  kept, each slide moves on by itself after it. **Use Timings** and **Play
  Narrations** set those flags of the show.
- **Insert → Screenshot** and **Insert → Icons** (below).

## In more than one app

- **Insert → Screenshot** (Documents, Presentations): Office's gallery of the
  other open windows, and the screens, each taken at its own size only when
  picked and put in like any picture. The window that asked is left out of
  the list.
- **Insert → Icons** (Documents, Worksheets, Presentations): the suite's own
  icons (the set the ribbon draws with), found by name and put in as a
  picture in the colour chosen. Nothing is fetched.

## Underneath

- One reader and one designer of a theme part serve Worksheets and
  Documents alike.
- A slide's scene is read again when the deck's order changes, for a slide
  whose action goes to another slide.
