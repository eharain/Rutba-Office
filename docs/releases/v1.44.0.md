A screen recorder in Video, layers in Image, and Edit in Image from Documents, Worksheets and Presentations.

## Video

- **Record Screen**: on the Home and Insert tabs and the start screen. A
  whole screen, one window, or a region drawn on the picture, kept as a
  crop you can change later. The camera records at the same time as a
  second file on the same clock, in a corner as a circle, a rounded or a
  square rectangle, small, medium or large, mirrored if you like, and
  dragged to its corner before you start. The microphone and the
  computer's own sound each have a level meter that moves before and
  during the recording; the computer's sound is recorded on Windows.
- **Quality**: 15, 30 or 60 frames a second, a size of up to 720, 1080 or
  1440 lines, and how sharp the picture is; the defaults keep a 1080p
  screen at 30 frames a second near 6 Mbps.
- **While recording**: a countdown of three (or five, ten, or none), Pause
  that holds everything at once, and Ctrl+Shift+F10 to stop and
  Ctrl+Shift+F9 to pause from any program, or the tray icon. The recording
  goes to disk a few megabytes at a time into a Rutba Recordings folder in
  Videos; the box says how long the disk has room for and warns when five
  minutes are left. If Rutba Office stops while you record, the launcher's
  Recover brings the recording back at its full length.
- **On Stop** the screen lands on the main track, the camera on the track
  above as a picture in picture you can move, resize, reshape or remove
  from its properties, and each sound on a track of its own.
- Presentations' Insert, Screen Recording opens the same recorder without
  the camera and still puts the recording on the slide. The launcher's
  Start something offers Record a screen.
- **Fixed**: playing a video in 1.43.0 flickered and its sound started over
  and over on top of itself. Each time the preview's own decoder finished a
  seek, the preview restarted, which sought again, some thirty times a
  second. A seek now only repaints, and the preview plays on.
- **Fixed**: a video exported from Video no longer starts with a moment of
  black under its titles while its first clip gets going. The export drew
  nothing while a clip was being sought; it now draws the frame it held,
  which also ends the black flash while scrubbing the preview.

## Image

- **Layers, inside the same app**: a picture still opens for quick edits
  as before. Insert puts text, WordArt, shapes, icons, charts, another
  picture or an adjustment on a layer of its own, and the picture you
  opened becomes the Background layer, which the quick edits still change.
- **The Layers panel** lists the layers from the top. Drag a row to reorder
  it, or use Bring Forward, Send Backward, Bring to Front and Send to Back.
  The eye hides a layer, the padlock locks it, a double-click renames it,
  and Group makes several layers move, show and fade as one. Each layer has
  an opacity and a blend mode (Multiply, Screen, Overlay, Soft light and
  twelve more). On the canvas a click picks a layer, a drag moves it, a
  corner handle resizes it and the round handle turns it, in steps of 15
  degrees with Shift.
- **Adjustment layers** (Punch, Matte, Warm, Cool, Mono, Fade or Noir, with
  their sliders) change every layer under them and none above them.
- **Canvas Size** adds room round the picture or cuts it from any of nine
  anchors without scaling anything, **Image Size** scales the whole
  picture, and **Trim** cuts away clear edges.
- **Projects**: Save writes a .rimg file with every picture inside it, so
  it opens again exactly as it was. While a layered picture is unsaved, a
  copy is kept and Recover offers it back after a crash. Export flattens
  the layers to PNG, JPEG or WebP with the same options as before.

## Documents, Worksheets and Presentations

- **Picture Format, Edit in Image**: select a picture and it opens in
  Image. Crop it, correct it, draw on it or add layers, press Save and
  return, and the edited picture goes back where it was, as wide as before
  and as tall as its new shape. Undo in the document brings the old one
  back. Worksheets and Presentations gain a Picture Format tab for it, with
  Arrange beside it.
- **Fixed**: in Worksheets a picture inserted, then undone and redone,
  lost its bytes.
