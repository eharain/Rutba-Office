Sixty more buttons work: the Draw tab in all three apps, video, audio and narration in Presentations, Compare and Split in Documents, and SmartArt everywhere among them.

## Documents

- **Draw tab.** Pen and Highlighter with their colours and thicknesses, Add,
  the Eraser, the Lasso and Ink to Shape, on the page in Print Layout. Each
  stroke is kept as a freeform floating in front of the words where it was
  drawn, named Ink N, which Word opens as a drawing.
- **References → Mark Citation** (Alt+Shift+I) stays open beside the page as
  Word's does (the citation, its category and short form, Mark, Mark All
  and Next Citation), and **Insert Table of Authorities** previews and
  writes a table per category, with **Update Table** beside it.
- **Review → Compare.** The original picked with Browse, this document as the
  revised one: a new document, in a window of its own, with what changed
  marked as revisions (words taken out and put in within a paragraph,
  paragraphs removed or added whole).
- **View → Split.** The document in two panes, one over the other: the lower
  one brought up to date as you type and scrolled on its own, a click in it
  putting the caret there above, the bar between them dragged to share the
  window.
- **Insert → Object.** A new blank document, worksheet or presentation, or an
  Office document from this computer, embedded as Word embeds one (the file
  whole, shown as its icon) and opened in its own app by a double-click.
  What opens is a copy: changes made there do not go back into the document.
- **Insert → Signature Line.** The suggested signer's name, title and e-mail,
  instructions and the sign date, written as Word writes them. The page now
  draws VML pictures, an older Word's among them, which it left out before.
- **Insert → SmartArt** (below).
- The ¶ that formatting marks show takes no room on its line; a page number
  right-aligned at the margin no longer drops onto a line of its own.

## Presentations

- **Insert → Video and Audio.** A video from this computer at its own shape
  with a poster frame taken from it, or a sound as a speaker, each with a
  play bar; in the show a click plays it without moving the show on, with
  its controls when **Show Media Controls** is on.
- **Screen Recording** (Insert and Record): a window or a screen picked from
  the Screenshot gallery, recorded with a bar showing the time, and put on
  the slide as a video.
- **Record.** The show recorded with the microphone, From Beginning or From
  Current Slide, with Pause and Stop: each slide's voice kept as its
  narration and its time as its timing, as PowerPoint keeps them. Preview,
  Clear and Audio beside it. **Export to Video** plays the deck into a file
  in Full HD, HD or Standard (MP4 where the machine makes one, WebM where it
  cannot).
- **Transitions → Sound.** No Sound, Stop Previous Sound, one of the suite's
  own sounds (heard as it is picked) or a WAV of yours, written as
  PowerPoint writes it.
- **Draw tab.** Pen, Pencil and Highlighter, Add, the Eraser, the Lasso, the
  Ruler, Ink to Shape, Ink Replay and Hide Ink; strokes kept as freeform
  shapes named Ink N.
- **Insert → Zoom.** Summary, Section and Slide Zooms: a picture of each slide
  or section picked, linked to it, which any PowerPoint follows. In the show
  here a click goes there and the next step comes back; PowerPoint's own
  zoom effect is not written.
- **Slide Show → Custom Slide Show.** The deck's named shows played, and
  made, edited, copied and removed in PowerPoint's dialog.
- **View → Notes Master and Handout Master**, each with a tab of its own, and
  **Slide Master → Insert Slide Master** for a second master.
- **Insert → WordArt** in PowerPoint's gallery; a run keeps its outline, no
  fill, glow and shadow.
- **Insert → Object**, as in Documents, and **View → View Direction**, which
  turns the window right to left.
- **Insert → SmartArt** and **Home → Convert to SmartArt** (below).

## Worksheets

- **Insert → Equation.** Typed in its linear form, set over the sheet as
  math, written as Excel writes one, and opened again by a double-click.
- **Insert → WordArt**, and a double-click on a shape opens its words.
- **Draw tab.** Pen and Highlighter, Add, the Eraser, the Lasso and Ink to
  Shape over the cells.
- **Insert → SmartArt** (below).

## In more than one app

- **Insert → SmartArt** (Documents, Worksheets, Presentations): Basic Block
  List, Vertical Box List, Basic Process, Basic Chevron Process, Basic Cycle
  and Hierarchy, drawn from lines typed in the text pane: a Tab in for a
  line under the one above. It goes in as one group of shapes in the theme's
  first accent holding their words, which Word, Excel and PowerPoint open
  and edit. It is not a SmartArt part, so Office's SmartArt tools do not act
  on it. In Presentations, **Convert to SmartArt** makes a box's lines a
  diagram where the box stood.

## Underneath

- A shape holding words in a Word group (an oval, a rounded box, a
  chevron) is painted as its shape with its words over it, on the page and
  in print; a line in a group straight down or across is drawn whole, in
  Documents and Worksheets.
- Check runs draw at a 100% display's scale whatever the machine's own, so a
  laptop's 125% panel no longer fails checks the code passes; the Zoom and
  Evaluate Formula checks wait for what they read.
