Word, Excel and PowerPoint files of every age open in full, references coloured and pointed at as a formula is typed, data bars and icon sets in the cells, tables in their table style and the preset shapes as their outlines on slides, and a selected drawing goes with Delete in every app.

## Files

- **Old Word documents open in full**: Word 97 to 2003, Word 6.0/95, Word 2.0
  and 1.0, Write and DOS Word. They keep their paragraphs and styles, character
  looks in the document's own code page, lists, tables, links, footnotes,
  headers and footers, pictures, and floating pictures, text boxes and
  shapes where Word put them, laid out by the layout rules Word kept in the
  file. Each is saved as a .docx.
- **Old Excel workbooks open in full**: Excel 2.1 to 2003. They keep
  values and formulas with their last results, names, number formats, fonts, fills and
  borders in the exact colours Excel 2007 and later kept for them, rich
  text, merged cells, frozen panes, column widths and row heights,
  hyperlinks, notes, pictures, charts (their kinds, stacking, axes,
  labels, legends and colours), shapes with their words, tables with their
  style and totals row, and conditional formats (colour scales, data bars,
  icon sets and Excel 97's rules with their formatting). Each is saved as an
  .xlsx.
- **Old PowerPoint presentations open in full**: PowerPoint 97 to 2003. They
  keep each slide's background, shapes in their fills, gradients, outlines and
  shadows with their words, pictures, lines, tables, the notes, a slide
  hidden from the show, its transition and its shapes' effects.
- **A .ppt or .xls saved by Office 2007 or later opens as it was made.**
  Office keeps a copy of the newer description inside such a file (the
  theme, the slide master and its layouts, and every shape as it was drawn),
  and that copy is what is opened: slides on their own layouts with real
  placeholders, shapes with every effect they had. A drawing an older
  Office changed since is read from the older records it changed.

## Worksheets

- **Pointing at cells while typing a formula**, as in Excel. Each reference
  in the formula is coloured (in the cell, in the formula bar and round the
  cells it names), the one at the caret drawn heavier. After `=`, a bracket,
  a comma or an operator, a click puts the cell's reference in, a drag makes
  a range, Shift+click stretches it; the arrow keys point in an edit begun
  by typing, and F4 turns the dollar signs.
- **Data bars and icon sets are drawn in their cells**: a bar under its
  number, as long as its share of the range (faded or solid, with its
  border, as Excel 2010's rule says), and an icon at the cell's left in the
  arrow, light, sign, symbol, flag, rating, quarter, star, triangle or box
  its value earns.
- **A heart, a smiley, callouts, block arrows, a can, a cloud and the
  flowchart shapes** are drawn as their outlines; each was a box of its
  size.
- **Stacked line and area charts** are drawn stacked, and an axis title is
  no longer taken for the chart's title.

## Presentations

- **Tables are drawn in their table style**: the header row in the accent
  with bold light words and a rule under it, the rows banded, light lines
  between the cells, a cell's own fill over the style's (tables from
  Insert → Table among them).
- **The preset shapes** (heart, smiley, callouts, block arrows, can, cube,
  donut, frame, stars, cloud and the flowchart shapes) are drawn as their
  outlines rather than as a box.

## Fixed

- Every app: a selected shape, chart or picture did not go with Delete; in
  Documents one from Insert was refused, in Presentations nothing happened
  until the slide had been clicked, and Worksheets' right-click on a drawing
  offered no Delete.
- Worksheets: a sheet with an Excel 2010 data bar lost its sparklines, and
  putting a table, a picture, a validation or a hyperlink on it wrote a file
  Excel had to repair.
- Worksheets: a sheet's first sparkline was written before its margins, not
  at its end.
- Presentations: Escape left Edit Points and let go of the shape too.
