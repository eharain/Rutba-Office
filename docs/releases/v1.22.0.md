A slide's words strike through, space out, change case and highlight; Layout and Reset on the Home tab.

## Presentation

- **Home → Layout and Reset.** Layout puts the slide on another of the
  deck's layouts from a menu, as the Designs pane does; Reset puts moved
  placeholders back where the layout has them. Both were greyed out.
- **Strikethrough, character spacing, change case and highlight.** The
  four Font controls are live: Strikethrough toggles, AV offers very
  tight to very loose, Aa rewrites the letters (sentence, lower, UPPER,
  Each Word), and the highlight offers four colours or none. Written as
  PowerPoint keeps them and drawn on the stage — the line through the
  words, the colour behind them. Caps and super/subscript a slide already
  had survive an edit now too.

- **Align text, Text direction and Columns.** The box's own three: the
  words sit at the top, middle or bottom of the box, run horizontally or
  turned up or down, and fill one, two or three columns, one to its foot
  before the next. Written on the body as PowerPoint keeps them, drawn on
  the stage the same way. The three buttons were greyed out.

## Rutba Word

- **Home → Shading and Borders.** A colour behind the paragraph from six
  light shades or none, and a rule under, over, beside or all round it.
  Written as Word keeps them (the paragraph's own shading and borders, in
  schema order), painted at once, printed and exported to PDF the same
  way. The two buttons were greyed out; shading and borders in a file were
  drawn on screen only.

## Worksheets

- **Data → Text to Columns and Remove Duplicates.** Text to Columns splits
  each cell of one selected column on a comma, tab, semicolon or space
  into the cells to its right, as Excel does (and refuses a wider
  selection with a sentence). Remove Duplicates takes the selection, or
  the block round the cell, drops every row that repeats an earlier one,
  leaves a header the block has alone and closes the rest up. Each is one
  undo step. Both buttons were greyed out.
- **Data → Sort by several columns.** A dialog with up to three levels,
  each a column of the block and A to Z or Z to A; the first decides and
  the next break its ties, blanks last either way, stable, one undo step.
  The A→Z and Z→A buttons sort by the active column as before.

  twelfth line of a two-column body drawn in the second column. One window
  check picks Middle and Rotate all text 270° and reads both on the body
  and the turn on the stage (`bullets`).
- Two engine tests write a paragraph's shading and two borders, find them
  in schema order, read them back after a save, take them off again and
  undo. Two window checks pick Light yellow and Bottom border on the first
  paragraph, read the paint, save, and read the paragraph properties back
  from the file (`RUTBA_VERIFY_ONLY=look`, capture word-look.png).
- Three engine tests split a column and refuse two, remove repeated rows

  the document ops, split the one from the Data tab, remove the other,
