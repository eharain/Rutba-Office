## Rutba Word

- **A paragraph of only pictures prints them side by side**, as many to a
  row as fit the column, each row whole and placed by the paragraph's
  alignment — as the screen has drawn them since 1.17.1 and as Word does.
  A sheet of four scanned cards prints two by two on one page rather than
  one under another across two. Under words a picture is still a row of
  its own.

- **A footnote sits at the foot of the page its reference lands on**, on
  screen as it has in print: under the body, above the bottom margin, under
  a short rule. The footnotes were one block at the end of the document,
  however many pages it ran to. The layout pass now reserves room at a
  page's foot for the notes referenced on it, so the body stops above them;
  a paragraph split across pages keeps each note with the line that
  references it; and a note whose reference moves follows it. In web,
  draft, read and outline layout the notes stay under the body; endnotes
  still end the document.

## Proven, not promised

- The window check that inserts a footnote reads it at the foot of the
  page, and a new one reads it on the reference's sheet, below the body's
  last line, on the bottom margin, with the hidden measuring copy hidden.
- An engine test prints four cards in a paragraph of their own two to a
  row on one page, and the same cards under words one to a row.

## Under the hood

- `pages.js` measures `.wd-notes-measure .wd-note[data-note]`, keeps a
  reserved height per page, answers `notes` (note id → page) and counts a
  change in it as a layout change; `PageNotes` draws each page's notes in
  `.wd-pagenotes`, a flex column aligned to the foot of the body area; a
  footnote reference carries `data-kind` and `data-id`.
