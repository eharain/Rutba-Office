Worksheets gets Excel's ribbon.

1.3.x gave Word every tab Microsoft Word has. This release does the same for
Worksheets: Home, Insert, Draw, Page Layout, Formulas, Data, Review, View,
Automate and Help, every group Excel has in each, laid out where Excel lays
it out. What the engine can do is wired; what it cannot is drawn in its
place, disabled, with a title that says exactly why — so a person arriving
from Excel finds every button where they expect it and is never left
guessing whether a feature exists.

## What is new on the ribbon

- **Home.** Format Painter, font size up and down, eleven border presets,
  Merge & Centre, Increase and Decrease Decimal (General becomes 0.0, 0.00
  becomes 0.000), **Cell Styles** — Excel's own: Heading 1–3, Title, Total,
  Good, Bad, Neutral, Input, Calculation, Check Cell, Note, Warning Text,
  Currency, Percent, Comma — Insert/Delete/Format menus (row height, column
  width, AutoFit, protect, lock), Fill Down and Fill Right (Ctrl+D, Ctrl+R),
  Clear Contents / Clear Formats, Sort & Filter, Find & Select with **Go To**
  (Ctrl+G) by cell reference or defined name.
- **Insert.** PivotTable, shapes, six chart kinds, the filter, **Text Box**,
  **Symbol**, Define Name.
- **Formulas.** **Insert Function** — Excel's categories (Financial, Logical,
  Text, Date & Time, Lookup & Reference, Math & Trig, Statistical) with a
  search; picking one starts `=NAME(` in the cell. Use in Formula lists the
  workbook's names. **Show Formulas** (Ctrl+`) shows every formula instead
  of its result. Calculate Now.
- **Data.** From Text/CSV, Refresh All, sort, filter, Data Validation,
  What-If Analysis (Goal Seek, Data Table), the CSV/TSV/PDF exports.
- **Review.** Workbook Statistics, Protect Sheet, lock and unlock cells,
  conditional formatting and validation.
- **View.** Gridlines, Headings and Formula Bar toggles; Zoom levels; New
  Window; **Freeze Panes** as a menu — at the selection, top row, first
  column, unfreeze; Full Screen.
- **Page Layout.** Margins, Orientation and Size (kept for the PDF export),
  gridlines and headings.
- **Help.** Help, Feedback, Keyboard Shortcuts, About.

## A workbook that would not open

A tender's requirements workbook — 24 sheets, 2.44 million cells, all but a
few thousand of them formatted blanks — took **eleven minutes** to open.
Building the calculation model asked the file for every cell by reference,
and each ask re-parsed the cell's whole row: quadratic in a sheet's width. The
model is now built by walking each row once, skipping a blank before its
attributes are looked at; the same workbook opens in **six seconds** and
holds a third of the memory. `Workbook.cells(sheet)` is the walk, and a
unit test keeps a 24,000-cell sheet under a second.

## Honest about the rest

Draw (ink), Themes, Print Area and print titles, Scale to Fit, Sparklines,
Slicers, cell comments, cell hyperlinks, Text to Columns, Flash Fill, Remove
Duplicates, Consolidate, outlining and subtotals, formula auditing arrows,
Page Break Preview, Automate scripts and Macros are all drawn where Excel
draws them, disabled, each with its reason. Macros will stay that way: a
spreadsheet that runs code it received in an email is how ransomware starts.
