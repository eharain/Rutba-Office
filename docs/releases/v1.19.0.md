## Rutba Word

- **A floating text box prints beside the words**, as it has shown on
  screen: a pull quote or a sidebar anchored at the left or the right is
  placed at its paragraph's top on that side, the lines beside it laid out
  shorter for as far down as it reaches, in print and PDF. It was drawn
  under its paragraph as a block. A box that would leave the lines no room
  stays under the words.

## Proven, not promised

- One engine test anchors a box at the right and at the left, reads it as a
  float with the lines beside it shorter and none running into it, and
  prints it.

## Under the hood

- `paginate.js` places a `floatbox` fragment (side, top, size, fill, line,
  laid paragraphs) and adds it to the page's floats; `pdf.js` draws it
  through `drawTextBox` at the float's place.
