Rutba Office 2.14.1 fixes Mail's automatic-replies banner, which in 2.14.0 took a whole column of the window.

## Mail

- **The banner floats.** While automatic replies are on, the line that
  says so, with Turn off, sits over the foot of the window. In 2.14.0 it
  took a column on the left and pushed your folders and messages aside.
