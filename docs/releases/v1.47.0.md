Text recognition on this computer: scanned PDFs made searchable, the words in a picture copied, and a table in a photo turned into cells, in six languages with the network off.

## Text recognition

- **Read on this computer, sent nowhere.** The suite now reads the words
  in pictures and scanned pages itself, with the network off and no
  account. It reads English, Urdu, Arabic, Hindi, Chinese and Japanese,
  and each language the suite learns will follow. Urdu is marked beta: it
  reads Naskh type well, but not yet Nastaliq print reliably.
- **Nothing waits for it.** Reading runs on threads of its own, one page
  a thread and never more than four, so no window is held while a long
  scan is read, and a reading can be cancelled.
- The reader is Tesseract 5 compiled to WebAssembly, with its six language
  models in the download and nothing fetched when it is first used.
  Around it the page work is our own: straightening a crooked scan, evening
  out unevenly lit paper, finding lines, columns and tables, and putting
  right-to-left lines in reading order.
- `docs/OCR.md` gives the measured error rates, speed and memory for each
  language, on pages drawn with known text and on real scans: about a
  second a page on a four-core laptop, English and Hindi real scans at
  2.3 and 2.7 per cent of characters wrong.

## PDF

- **Recognise Text** finds the words in scanned pages and writes them in
  as an invisible layer under each page, so Find, selecting and copying
  work on scans. The pages look exactly as they did. Choose all pages, the
  selected ones or this one, and the languages; pages that already have
  text are left alone. A progress bar counts the pages, with Cancel.
- The layer is added after the file's original bytes, which stay exactly
  as they were, and Undo takes it away.
- Fixed: a new search now moves to its first match when that match is on
  another page; a damaged file whose page list is not a list opens with no
  pages instead of failing.
- Not yet: pages that mix text with a scan are skipped, and a scan turned
  sideways is not turned back.

## Pictures and Image

- **Copy Text from Picture** puts every word in a picture on the
  clipboard in reading order, and lays the recognised lines over the
  picture so a drag selects and copies just the words wanted. A bar says
  how many lines were found, with Copy All and Hide.
- In Pictures it is on the Home tab and on the viewer's bar under the
  picture; the lines follow the picture as it is zoomed, scrolled or
  turned. In Image it is on the Home tab and reads the picture as edited.

## Worksheets

- **Data from Picture** (Data, From Picture, from a file or the
  clipboard) reads a printed table into cells. The picture is shown beside
  a preview of the cells, each one editable, with the cells the reading
  was unsure of marked to check. Insert Data puts them in at the selected
  cell as one step Undo takes back, numbers as numbers.
- Tables with ruling lines and tables without are both read; a cell the
  first pass missed is read again on its own.
- Not yet: a figure such as 4.50 lands as 4.5.

## Checked

- Engine tests read a page in each of the six languages and check every
  word's box; prove reading happens on the pool's threads and never the
  caller's, that queued and running readings cancel, and that a reading
  makes no network request; and check skew to a tenth of a degree,
  unevenly lit paper, ruling lines, columns, and ruled and unruled tables
  read cell for cell. The PDF text layer's tests prove the original bytes
  stay the start of the file, for both kinds of cross-reference, object
  streams, inherited resources, rotated pages and right-to-left words.
- Window checks: `ocr-pdf` (9) finds English and Urdu words on scanned
  pages, selects and copies them, undoes and redoes the layer and reads
  the saved file's start back; `ocr-picture` (10) copies and selects in
  Pictures and Image through zoom and edits; `ocr-sheet` (5) inserts a
  table from a picture with numbers as numbers.
- A build of the installer read a page with the engine and models
  unpacked beside the application archive.
- The fuzzer damaged 5,400 files at seed 4713: 2,565 opened and 2,835
  refused in a sentence, none faulted. The PDF reader's fuzzer damaged 900
  PDFs at seed 4714; its first run found one fault, a page list left as a
  number, now read as no pages. Run again: 797 read and 103 refused in a
  sentence, none faulted, none hung.
